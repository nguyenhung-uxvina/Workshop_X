# E-Myth Revisited — Franchise Thinking Validates WX Skill Infrastructure

> Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bwXS6SFV5RE

## Core Thesis (Michael Gerber)

Hầu hết small business fail vì owner = technician (doer), không phải entrepreneur (system builder). Giải pháp: xây SOPs cho MỌI process → business chạy không phụ thuộc 1 người.

## 3 Identities

| Identity | Role | WX CEO hiện tại |
|----------|------|----------------|
| Entrepreneur | Visionary, tương lai | ~30% thời gian (strategy, FORGE) |
| Manager | Organizer, operations | ~20% (BRIDGE, SOPs) |
| Technician | Doer, hands-on | ~50% (design, research, engineering) |

**E-Myth says:** Shift from Technician → Entrepreneur. CEO should spend most time ON the business, not IN the business.

## 7 Blueprints → WX Mapping

| Blueprint | WX Implementation | Status |
|-----------|------------------|--------|
| 1. SOPs | 59 Claude Skills + 15 SOPs | STRONG |
| 2. Financial systems | Minimal — no P&L in vault | GAP |
| 3. Delegation | COD system + AI delegation | UNIQUE (AI, not humans) |
| 4. Brand | ACH thesis + defense positioning | On track |
| 5. Franchise thinking | Skills = franchise manual cho AI | STRONG |
| 6. Team | Solo CEO + AI agents | GAP — hire plan needed |
| 7. Sellable business | IRONMESH IP + documented methodology | On track |

## Key Validation

**WX đã build "franchise" cho AI agent mà không biết.** 59 skills = 59 franchise manuals. Mỗi skill = SOP mà bất kỳ Claude instance nào cũng chạy được. E-Myth says "build so anyone can run it" — WX đã làm điều này cho AI.
