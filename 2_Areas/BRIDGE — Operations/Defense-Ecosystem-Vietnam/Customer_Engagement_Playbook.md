# Customer Engagement Playbook — Workshop X

> **Context:** WX đã giao 1,064 units cho VN military. Document này codify cách engage customers systematically, không phải cold-start.
> **Owner:** CEO (Core — judgment required). BD support = Offload.

---

## 1. ENGAGEMENT PRINCIPLES

1. **Listen more than talk** — understand pain points before presenting solutions
2. **Relationship-first** — long-term trust > short-term sale
3. **Connect to pain** — every capability presented must link to a stated customer problem
4. **Follow through** — deliver what you promise within stated timeline
5. **Respect hierarchy** — VN defense ecosystem has clear chains of command

---

## 2. MEETING FRAMEWORK — UTT/NEW PRODUCT ENGAGEMENT

### 2.1 Pre-Meeting Checklist

- [ ] Attendees researched (rank, role, decision influence)
- [ ] WX credentials document prepared (bilingual)
- [ ] Product technical brief prepared (bilingual)
- [ ] Success stories / deployed references ready (1,064 units shipped)
- [ ] Clear asks + offers defined
- [ ] Next steps prepared in advance
- [ ] Meeting room/logistics confirmed

### 2.2 Meeting Flow (90 minutes)

**Minutes 0-10 — Relationship Context**
- Introductions, WX background (brief — they likely know us)
- Confirm mutual connections, shared history
- Set tone: collaborative, respectful, exploratory
- Reference past successful deliveries if appropriate

**Minutes 10-30 — Customer Current State + Pain Points**
- Ask about current training / operational challenges
- Understand current equipment (imports, ad-hoc solutions)
- Identify pain points: cost, availability, realism, flexibility, logistics
- Training frequency + desired scenarios
- **KEY: Listen, take notes, don't interrupt**

**Minutes 30-60 — Capability Presentation**
- Technical capabilities (brief, focused on THEIR stated pain points)
- Demonstrate relevance to problems they just described
- Show deployed references (photos, data from 1,064 units)
- Recovery/reusability if applicable (cost savings)
- Operational integration concept
- **Connect EACH feature to a specific pain point heard**

**Minutes 60-75 — Discussion**
- Customer questions → answer honestly (say "chưa biết" if unsure)
- Clarifications on specs, timeline, pricing ballpark
- Concerns addressed directly
- Regulatory / procurement process discussion

**Minutes 75-90 — Next Steps**
- Summarize mutual interest
- Schedule next meeting (specific date if possible)
- Assign action items per side
- Documents to exchange
- Thank customer for time

## 3. STAKEHOLDER-SPECIFIC APPROACHES

### 3.1 Operational Commanders

**What they care about:** Mission effectiveness, operational fit, training realism
**Language:** Outcome-focused, mission-oriented
**Cadence:** Quarterly meetings
**Do:** Show field data, operational scenarios, user testimonials
**Don't:** Lead with specs or pricing

### 3.2 Technical Procurement (Cục Vũ khí, Cục Kỹ thuật)

**What they care about:** Specifications compliance, quality, cost, documentation
**Language:** Technical, specification-oriented, formal
**Cadence:** Monthly during procurement, quarterly otherwise
**Do:** Provide detailed specs, test data, compliance matrices
**Don't:** Be vague about specifications or timelines

### 3.3 Training Command

**What they care about:** Curriculum integration, trainer effectiveness, realistic scenarios
**Language:** Pedagogical, scenario-driven
**Cadence:** Regular collaboration (monthly+)
**Do:** Show training outcome data, offer trial/demo periods
**Don't:** Oversell — promise what the product actually delivers

### 3.4 Strategic Command

**What they care about:** Long-term capability, regional positioning, budget justification
**Language:** Strategic, multi-year horizon, capability roadmap
**Cadence:** Annual + major decision points
**Do:** Present roadmap, show how WX products compound capability over time
**Don't:** Overwhelm with technical details
