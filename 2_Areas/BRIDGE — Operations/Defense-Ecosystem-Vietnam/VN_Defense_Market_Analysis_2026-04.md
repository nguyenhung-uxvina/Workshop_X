- 3,260 km coastline + Biển Đông territorial disputes ongoing
- VN investing in Bastion-P coastal defense + Gepard-class frigates
- Maritime domain awareness + sovereignty enforcement = national priority
- Modernization priority across all services
- Preference for domestic industry solutions

## 4. PROCUREMENT PATTERNS

### 4.1 Typical Defense Procurement Cycle (VN)

```
1. Requirements identification (12-24 months)
2. Budget allocation (12 months)
3. Procurement process (12-24 months)
4. Execution (1-5 years)
5. Operations + support (10-30 years)
```

**Total: 3-7 years for new products. WX advantage: existing products skip steps 1-3.**

### 4.2 VN-Specific Considerations

- **Domestic preference** — WX = QPAN status, major advantage
- **Multi-source avoidance** — prefer not to depend on single foreign supplier
- **Political alignment** — required, WX = aligned
- **Long-term relationships** — valued highly, WX = proven over multiple years
- **Documentation + transparency** — expected, extensive
- **Quality certification** — required (ISO 9001 target Q3 2027)
- **After-sales support** — strong commitment expected

### 4.3 Common Procurement Hurdles

| Hurdle | WX Preparedness |
