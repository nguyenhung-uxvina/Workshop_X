## 2. CRITICAL ROLES — DETAILED PROFILES

### 2.1 Software Architect (Phase 3 — revenue-gated)

**Why deferred vs vision doc (which says Q1-Q2 2026):**
- WX Phase 1 products (cash cows + UTT-Towed) don't require full LCS stack
- $120-180K/yr loaded cost = 25-35% of current revenue — not sustainable
- VN-XUONG-UUV LARS can use simplified control without ROS 2
- Hire when MSM/USV revenue justifies the investment

**When to activate:** Revenue > $8M/yr AND MSM production decision made

**Profile (from vision doc, preserved):**
- 10+ years software architecture experience
- 5+ years safety-critical / mission-critical systems
- Real-time systems, embedded Linux, RTOS
- Strong C++, ROS 2 preferred
- Naval/maritime experience strong advantage
- Willingness to work in Vietnam

**Sourcing channels:**
1. Vietnamese diaspora (US/EU defense contractors)
2. International recruitment (defense/aerospace firms)
3. Regional (Singapore, Malaysia, Korea, Japan)
4. Senior consultant as interim (if architecture decisions urgent)

**Evaluation:**
- Phase 1: Resume + phone screen (HR)
- Phase 2: Architecture discussion 60min (CEO)
- Phase 3: Leadership assessment (CEO)
- Phase 4: References (3-5 minimum)

**Green flags:** Safety-critical experience, HW+SW integration, team building track record
**Red flags:** Pure software (no hardware awareness), no safety-critical, technology-stack dogmatic

## 4. RETENTION STRATEGY

### 4.1 Compensation
- Market-competitive base salary
- Performance bonuses (project completion, production targets)
- Profit sharing for senior roles (when profitable)

### 4.2 Meaningful Work
- Real-world impact: VN maritime security
- Technology challenges: multi-domain engineering
- Ownership: small team = visible contribution
- Strategic importance communicated regularly

### 4.3 Career Development
- Clear career paths (technician → engineer → senior → lead)
- Training budget per person
- Cross-product exposure (compound learning)
- Conference attendance for senior engineers

### 4.4 Culture
- Engineering excellence focus
- Methodology discipline (Pahl-Beitz)
- Team-first, no-blame
- Respectful communication
- Reasonable hours

### 4.5 Retention Targets
- Year 1: 85%+
- Year 2-3: 90%+
- Senior retention: 90%+ year 1

### 5.3 Key Hire Sequencing Principle

```
Revenue grows → Production bottleneck appears → Hire production
↓
New product validated → Development capacity needed → Hire engineer
↓
Export market opens → BD capacity needed → Hire BD
↓
Technology complexity exceeds CEO capacity → Hire specialist
```

**Never hire in anticipation. Hire in response to validated demand.**
