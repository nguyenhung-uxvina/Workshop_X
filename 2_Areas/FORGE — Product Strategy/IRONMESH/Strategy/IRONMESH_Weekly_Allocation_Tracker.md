## CONSTRAINT NAMING DRILL

> Run this at the start of every Friday review. Target: <1 minute.

Ask: **"If I could fix one thing this week, what would make everything else faster?"**

Common answers and what they mean:
- "I couldn't start the VN-RANGE-001 deployment" → B2 (trust/procurement delay) is the constraint → more Level 3-4 touchpoints needed
- "I spent too much time on QC review" → S3 not automated enough → improve QC Gate
- "I kept getting pulled into other products" → B1 (complexity ceiling) active → tighten Freeze Order
- "I didn't practice the briefing" → B-NS investment not happening → schedule it like a meeting
- "I ran out of energy on Thursday" → B3 (Skill Depletion) activating → protect the 3h buffer

**If you cannot name the constraint in <1 min:** The problem is information flow (L6). Run `/cld` to map the system, then name it.
