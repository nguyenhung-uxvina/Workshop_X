# ASEAN Defense Market Overview — Workshop X Export Perspective

> **Timeline:** Export not before Phase 2 (2028+). Document = preparation for future BD.
> **Priority markets:** Philippines (#1), Indonesia (#2), Malaysia (#3), Thailand (#4).

---

## 1. REGIONAL CONTEXT

ASEAN governments placing premium on naval modernization. Key signals:
- Indonesia: massive fleet expansion planned (12 subs, 4 destroyers, 36 frigates)
- Philippines: $35B multi-year modernization, 2026 defense budget ~$5.2B (+16%)
- Regional response to Chinese maritime assertiveness
- Scale and pace of procurement clearly increasing

**WX positioning:** ITAR-free + ASEAN-based + training-focused + competitive pricing

---

## 2. COUNTRY PROFILES

### 2.1 Philippines — Priority #1

**Why #1:** Strongest anti-China alignment → highest ITAR-free value. Active submarine procurement. Growing unmanned systems interest (MANTAS T-12 USVs). Budget growing fast.

**Addressable opportunity (2028-2033):**

| Product | Annual Demand | Annual Value |
|---|---|---|
| UTT (MCM training) | 50-120 units | $1-3M |
| ATT (aerial targets) | 20-50 units | $0.5-1.5M |
| MSM platforms | 0-1 units | $0-3M |
| LARS retrofits | 1-3 systems | $0.5-1.5M |
| **Total** | | **$3-8M/yr** |

**Entry strategy:**
- ITAR-free + anti-China utility messaging
- ASEAN solidarity positioning
- Reasonable pricing vs Western alternatives
- Conference presence (ADAS Manila)

### 2.2 Indonesia — Priority #2

**Why #2:** Largest ASEAN archipelagic state → massive MCM needs. Interest in local construction + technology transfer. Huge fleet modernization planned.

**Fleet expansion targets:** 12 submarines (from 4), 4 destroyers (from 0), 36 frigates (from 9), 18 corvettes, 27 fast attack craft, 42 OPVs

**Addressable opportunity (2028-2033):**

| Product | Annual Demand | Annual Value |
|---|---|---|
| UTT (MCM/ASW training) | 80-200 units | $2-6M |
| ATT (aerial targets) | 30-60 units | $0.5-2M |
| MSM platforms | 0-2 units | $0-4M |
| LARS retrofits | 1-4 systems | $0.5-2M |
| **Total** | | **$4-12M/yr** |

**Entry strategy:**
- Local partnership (PT PAL or similar) — technology transfer offering
- Regional solidarity messaging
- Competitive pricing
- Conference presence (Indo Defence Jakarta)

### 2.3 Malaysia — Priority #3

**Why #3:** Active modernization (FA-50, Littoral Mission Ships). Growing maritime awareness. Moderate budget.

**Addressable opportunity:** $2-5M/yr
**Entry:** Training systems focus, regional partnership potential, LIMA Langkawi

### 2.4 Thailand — Priority #4

**Why #4:** Established defense industry, stable modernization. Competing with Korean + Chinese alternatives.

**Addressable opportunity:** $1-3M/yr
**Entry:** Training market accessible, competitive differentiation needed

### 2.5 Others

- **Singapore:** Advanced, premium Western systems. Potential partner (ST Engineering), limited direct opportunity.
- **Brunei:** Small navy, limited market.
- **Myanmar:** Political issues, international restrictions.
