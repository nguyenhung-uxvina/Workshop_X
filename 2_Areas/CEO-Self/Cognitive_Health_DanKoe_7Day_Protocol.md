# Dan Koe — 7-Day Creativity Recovery Protocol

> Source: https://x.com/thedankoe/status/2036824811712942576

## 3 Narrowers of the Mind

1. **Conditioning** — xã hội dạy nghĩ giống nhau
2. **Productivity as priority** — stressed mind chỉ survival mode
3. **Infinite input, zero processing** — mental metabolism quá tải

## 7-Day Protocol

### Day 1-2: Reduce Input Fast
- Work limit 4h/day (strict alarm)
- Cắt primary input source (podcast commute, scroll before bed)
- Walk không headphones, không phone

### Day 3-4: Digest What's There
- Đọc chậm 1 chapter → khi 1 câu hit → dừng, sit with it
- Ngồi im 10 phút (không app, không guided breathing)
- Walk + quan sát chi tiết (sidewalk, tree branches, sky)

### Day 5-6: Become Interested Again
- Trust ideas will come — đừng ghi chú mọi thứ
- 1 real conversation (không networking, không catching up)
- Extend walk — có thể không muốn dừng

### Day 7: Create From Abundance
- Make something with no plan (write, draw, voice note, cook)
- Don't share it — separate generative from evaluative
- After 24h: decide if worth sharing

## Key Mechanisms

- **DMN (Default Mode Network):** Only active when NOT consuming → source of insight, self-reflection, future imagining
- **Hedonic treadmill reversal:** Deprive → simple pleasures return → beginner's mind
- **Reticular Activating System:** Meaningful project = lens → brain surfaces relevant ideas automatically
