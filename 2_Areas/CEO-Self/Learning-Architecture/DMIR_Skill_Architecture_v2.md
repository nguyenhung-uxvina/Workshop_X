## I. RÀ SOÁT: DMIR HIỆN TẠI

### 1.1 DMIR Skill Hiện Tại Làm Gì

DMIR hiện tại là **một analytical framework 4-phase** cho defense manufacturing:

```
D (Diagnosis)    → Map system structure, identify archetypes (CLD)
M (Modeling)     → Quantify dynamics, identify constraint (SD model)
I (Intervention) → TOC Five Focusing Steps, exploit constraint
R (Reflection)   → AAR, challenge paradigms, plan next cycle
```

**Strengths:**
- Methodology nghiêm túc: Systems Dynamics + TOC + Meadows Leverage Points
- Defense-specific: low volume, high complexity, long lifecycle
- Progressive leverage targeting: L10→L7→L5→L3→L2 across cycles
- Vietnamese context aware

**Weaknesses (trước khi có bộ ba):**
- Standalone: không biết BRIDGE, FORGE, HELIX tồn tại
- Generic: "defense manufacturing" nhưng không map vào Workshop X's specific products
- Heavy: 28-week full cycle, quá dài cho 25h/week CEO capacity
- Analysis-biased: Phase 1-2 chiếm 6 tuần, Phase 3 thực hành chỉ 13 tuần
- Missing human-AI collaboration: giả định toàn bộ do team người làm

### 1.2 DMIR vs. Bộ Ba — Overlap Analysis

```
CHỒNG LẤN:

DMIR Phase 1 (Diagnosis)  ≈  BRIDGE: bridge-signal-extract + bridge-risk-radar
  "Map system structure"       "Extract signals from operations, detect risks"
  → CẢ HAI đều map hệ thống. Nhưng BRIDGE làm LIÊN TỤC, DMIR làm theo CYCLE.

DMIR Phase 2 (Modeling)    ≈  Không có equivalent trực tiếp
  "Build SD model, simulate"   → Đây là DMIR's unique value
  → Bộ ba chưa có modeling capability

DMIR Phase 3 (Intervention) ≈ FORGE + HELIX execution
  "TOC: exploit constraint"     "FORGE: ACH decisions; HELIX: design execution"
  → DMIR nói "intervene at bottleneck"; FORGE/HELIX nói "intervene HOW"

DMIR Phase 4 (Reflection)  ≈  BRIDGE: bridge-flywheel + bridge-cross-learn
  "AAR, paradigm challenge"     "Measure flywheel, cross-product learning"
  → CẢ HAI đều learn from outcomes. BRIDGE liên tục, DMIR theo cycle.
```

## II. ĐÁNH GIÁ: DMIR KHÔNG PHẢI FRAMEWORK THỨ 4

### 2.1 Insight Cốt Lõi

**DMIR không phải chiều thứ 4 của Compound Law. DMIR là META-LAYER — phương pháp tư duy hệ thống ÁP DỤNG LÊN cả ba framework.**

```
SAI (DMIR as 4th dimension):
  Workshop X Value = BRIDGE × FORGE × HELIX × DMIR
  → Thêm một số nhân → phức tạp hóa → CEO không track nổi

ĐÚNG (DMIR as meta-layer):
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  DMIR: HOW WE THINK ABOUT THE SYSTEM            │
  │  (Systems Thinking + Modeling + Constraint ID)   │
  │                                                   │
  │  Applied TO:                                      │
  │  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │
  │  │ BRIDGE: operations → diagnose ops bottleneck│ │
  │  │ FORGE: products → model ACH economics       │ │
  │  │ HELIX: design → find design process constraint│ │
  │  │ WHOLE SYSTEM: Compound Law optimization      │ │
  │  └─────────────────────────────────────────────┘ │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

DMIR = LENS qua đó CEO nhìn tất cả 3 frameworks
     = THINKING PROTOCOL cho mọi quyết định strategic
     = META-SKILL không phải operational skill
```

### 2.2 Tại Sao Phân Biệt Này Quan Trọng

```
Nếu DMIR = operational framework (song song BRIDGE/FORGE/HELIX):
  → CEO phải track 4 dashboards
  → Skills chồng lấn (DMIR Diagnosis ≈ BRIDGE signal-extract)
  → Team confused: "Dùng DMIR hay BRIDGE cho vấn đề vận hành?"
  → Compound Law thêm biến → khó calculate

Nếu DMIR = meta-layer (trên BRIDGE/FORGE/HELIX):
  → CEO dùng DMIR để CHUẨN ĐOÁN nên focus framework nào
  → Skills bổ sung (DMIR adds modeling capability bộ ba chưa có)
  → Team rõ: "DMIR cho strategic thinking; BRIDGE/FORGE/HELIX cho execution"
  → Compound Law giữ nguyên 3 biến
```

### 2.3 DMIR's Unique Value — Cái Bộ Ba Chưa Có

```
DMIR CUNG CẤP NHỮNG GÌ BỘ BA THIẾU:

1. FORMAL CONSTRAINT IDENTIFICATION (scientific, not intuitive)
   Bộ ba: CEO estimates "HELIX is bottleneck at 29%"
   DMIR adds: SD model PROVES HELIX is constraint bằng simulation
   → Giảm risk quyết định sai constraint

2. SYSTEM DYNAMICS MODELING (quantitative prediction)
   Bộ ba: qualitative dashboards (scores, trends)
   DMIR adds: quantitative models that PREDICT outcomes
   → "Nếu hire 2 AI engineers → FORGE score increases to X in Y months"

3. PROGRESSIVE LEVERAGE TARGETING (structured L10→L2 journey)
   Bộ ba: identifies leverage points nhưng ad hoc
   DMIR adds: systematic escalation across cycles
   → Ensures each quarter targets HIGHER leverage

4. PARADIGM CHALLENGE (formal protocol)
   Bộ ba: "Shifting the Burden" mentioned but no counter-protocol
   DMIR adds: structured paradigm assessment in Phase 4
   → Formal mechanism to question "xưởng cơ khí" identity
```

### SKILL 1: `dmir-map` — Causal Loop Diagram

**DMIR Phase:** Diagnosis
**Trigger:** "map system", "vẽ CLD", "causal loop", "feedback structure", "hệ thống này vận hành thế nào?"

**Workflow:**

```
Input: System/subsystem description (text, problem statement, or data)
  │
  ▼
Step 1: 🤖 Generate draft CLD
  │     ├── Identify stocks (what accumulates?)
  │     ├── Identify flows (what changes stocks?)
  │     ├── Map causal links (A → B: same direction? opposite?)
  │     ├── Identify loops: Reinforcing (R) and Balancing (B)
  │     ├── Mark delays (||)
  │     └── Output: ASCII or Mermaid CLD
  │
  ▼
Step 2: 👤 Validate & enrich
  │     ├── "Link này có đúng không? Trong thực tế A có dẫn đến B?"
  │     ├── "Thiếu stock nào? Flow nào bị ẩn?"
  │     ├── "Delay này bao lâu trong thực tế? Tuần? Tháng? Năm?"
  │     └── ★ "Hidden stock": stock nào QUAN TRỌNG nhưng KHÔNG AI NÓI ĐẾN?
  │
  ▼
Step 3: 🤖 Refine and annotate
  │     ├── Updated CLD with corrections
  │     ├── Loop inventory: each R and B loop named and characterized
  │     ├── Dominance analysis: "R1 currently dominant → growth"
  │     └── Flag: "B3 đang build pressure → sẽ dominate nếu..."
  │
  ▼
Output: Validated CLD + Loop inventory + Dominance analysis
```

**Scope Examples:**
```
System-wide:    "Map Workshop X operations" → CLD of Compound Law dynamics
BRIDGE-specific: "Map knowledge flow" → CLD of KB → Signal → Flywheel
FORGE-specific:  "Map ACH economics" → CLD of cost → validation → trust → compound
HELIX-specific:  "Map integration debt" → CLD of domain speeds → sync → debt → quality
```

**Human Must:**
- Validate causal links (AI can propose, but domain expert confirms reality)
- Identify hidden stocks (tacit knowledge, political capital, team morale)
- Estimate delays accurately (requires operational experience)

### SKILL 2: `dmir-archetype` — System Archetype Detection

**DMIR Phase:** Diagnosis
**Trigger:** "archetype", "tại sao vấn đề cứ lặp lại?", "pattern", "system trap", "shifting burden"

**Workflow:**

```
Input: CLD (from dmir-map) or problem description
  │
  ▼
Step 1: 🤖 Pattern match against 8 archetypes
  │     ├── Shifting the Burden (86% frequency in past analyses)
  │     │   "Fixing symptoms atrophies fundamental capability"
  │     │   Check: "Is there an easy fix that keeps recurring?"
  │     │
  │     ├── Fixes That Fail
  │     │   "Solution creates side effects that worsen original problem"
  │     │   Check: "Did our fix create a new problem?"
  │     │
  │     ├── Success to the Successful
  │     │   "Winner gets more resources, loser starves"
  │     │   Check: "Are resources flowing to already-strong projects?"
  │     │
  │     ├── Eroding Goals
  │     │   "Standards drop because 'that's how it is'"
  │     │   Check: "Have we accepted delays/defects as normal?"
  │     │
  │     ├── Escalation
  │     │   "Competitive arms race consumes resources"
  │     │   Check: "Are we matching competitor action-for-action?"
  │     │
  │     ├── Tragedy of the Commons
  │     │   "Shared resource depleted by individual optimization"
  │     │   Check: "Is CEO attention spread across all products equally?"
  │     │
  │     ├── Growth and Underinvestment
  │     │   "Growth hits ceiling because investment was deferred"
  │     │   Check: "Did we under-invest in talent when times were good?"
  │     │
  │     └── Policy Resistance
  │         "Multiple actors pursue conflicting goals"
  │         Check: "Engineering wants innovation, customer wants low cost?"
  │
  ▼
Step 2: 🤖 Generate archetype diagnosis
  │     ├── Which archetypes active? (usually 2-3)
  │     ├── Where in the archetype cycle are we?
  │     ├── What's the symptomatic vs. fundamental solution?
  │     └── Counter-strategy for each active archetype
  │
  ▼
Step 3: 👤 Validate with lived experience
  │     ├── "Does this match what you're seeing?"
  │     ├── "Which archetype feels most dangerous right now?"
  │     └── ★ Personal check: "Am I part of the archetype? (Analyst Trap = Shifting Burden)"
  │
  ▼
Output: Active archetype report + counter-strategies
```

**Workshop X Known Active Archetypes:**
```
🔴 Shifting the Burden — Analysis substituting for prototyping (Analyst Trap)
🔴 Growth and Underinvestment — AI talent gap limiting all FORGE activities
🟡 Eroding Goals — "Defense projects always run late" accepted as normal
🟡 Success to the Successful — V-SMASH gets attention, other products starve

### SKILL 3: `dmir-model` — System Dynamics Model

**DMIR Phase:** Modeling
**Trigger:** "model", "simulate", "mô phỏng", "nếu... thì sao?", "what-if", "predict"

**Workflow:**

```
Input: Validated CLD + historical data (if available)
  │
  ▼
Step 1: 🤖 Convert CLD to stock-flow model
  │     ├── Define stocks with initial values
  │     ├── Specify flows (equations or estimates)
  │     ├── Parameterize (from data or expert estimates)
  │     └── Output: Python/PySD model or structured specification
  │
  ▼
Step 2: 🤖 Validate model
  │     ├── Reproduce known historical behavior ("does it look right?")
  │     ├── Extreme condition test ("if demand 10x, what breaks?")
  │     ├── Sensitivity analysis ("which parameter matters most?")
  │     └── Flag: "model prediction contradicts intuition → investigate"
  │
  ▼
Step 3: 🤖→👤 Scenario simulation
  │     ├── Baseline: "if nothing changes, where are we in 12 months?"
  │     ├── Scenario A: "hire 2 AI engineers → what changes?"
  │     ├── Scenario B: "double prototype rate → what changes?"
  │     ├── Scenario C: "lose senior engineer → what happens?"
  │     └── Each scenario: timeline, bottleneck shift, cost, risk
  │
  ▼
Step 4: 👤 Interpret & decide
  │     ├── "Which scenario best matches our strategy?"
  │     ├── "Where does the model surprise me? Why?"
  │     └── ★ "Model is a tool, not truth. What does the model NOT capture?"
  │
  ▼
Output: SD model + scenario comparison + recommendations
```

**Applied to Compound Law:**
```
COMPOUND LAW MODEL:

Stocks:
  S_BRIDGE: knowledge_base_coverage (0-100%)
  S_FORGE: product_strategy_maturity (0-100%)
  S_HELIX: design_execution_rigor (0-100%)
  S_COMPOUND: S_BRIDGE × S_FORGE × S_HELIX

Flows:
  dS_BRIDGE/dt = f(signal_extraction_rate, KB_capture_effort, flywheel_speed)
  dS_FORGE/dt = f(ACH_decisions_made, validation_completed, library_growth)
  dS_HELIX/dt = f(sync_compliance, ICD_tracking, 6flow_adoption)

Cross-flows:
  S_BRIDGE → S_FORGE: KB feeds product decisions
  S_FORGE → S_HELIX: ACH decisions become design requirements
  S_HELIX → S_BRIDGE: design outcomes become organizational knowledge

SIMULATE: "If invest 60% effort in HELIX (bottleneck), 
           20% BRIDGE, 20% FORGE → compound value in 6 months?"
```

**Human Must:**
- Provide parameter estimates from operational experience
- Validate model behavior against known patterns
- Interpret scenarios in strategic context (model can't capture politics, relationships)

### SKILL 4: `dmir-constrain` — Scientific Constraint Identification

**DMIR Phase:** Modeling → Intervention bridge
**Trigger:** "constraint", "bottleneck", "chỗ nghẽn", "đâu là giới hạn?", "what's blocking us?"

**Workflow:**

```
Input: SD model (from dmir-model) or operational observation
  │
  ▼
Step 1: 🤖 Stress test
  │     ├── In model: increase demand/ambition 50%
  │     ├── Observe: which resource saturates first (>95% utilization)?
  │     ├── Observe: which stock accumulates (WIP, debt, backlog)?
  │     └── Preliminary constraint identification
  │
  ▼
Step 2: 👤 Ground truth verification (WALK THE FLOOR)
  │     ├── "Model says X is constraint. Is it ACTUALLY?"
  │     ├── Physical observation: is resource really at 95%+ utilization?
  │     ├── Interview: do people BEFORE constraint have excess capacity?
  │     ├── Interview: do people AFTER constraint wait for its output?
  │     └── ★ "Constraint is often NOT where people think it is"
  │
  ▼
Step 3: 🤖→👤 Constraint characterization
  │     ├── Type: Physical (equipment, space) / Policy (rules, process) / 
  │     │         Market (demand) / Knowledge (skills, information)
  │     ├── Location: which framework? BRIDGE / FORGE / HELIX?
  │     ├── Capacity: how much throughput does it limit?
  │     ├── Cost to elevate: what would it take to remove this constraint?
  │     └── If removed, what becomes NEXT constraint?
  │
  ▼
Output: Constraint Report → feeds dmir-lever for intervention design
```

**Workshop X Current Constraint Chain:**
```
CONSTRAINT CHAIN (most binding first):

1. AI TALENT (1-2 engineers)
   Type: Knowledge constraint
   Location: FORGE substrate → limits ALL FORGE skills
   Impact: Caps model development to ~2 products/quarter
   Elevate: Hire 2 more AI engineers (12 months to productive)

2. CEO ATTENTION (25h/week split across 6+ products)  
   Type: Policy/physical constraint
   Location: ALL frameworks (CEO is single integration point)
   Impact: Caps strategic decisions, sync meetings, reviews
   Elevate: Delegate Phase 0-1 decisions + formalize sync protocol

3. PHYSICAL PROTOTYPE RATE (dP/dt ≈ 0)
   Type: Policy/knowledge constraint
   Location: HELIX → limits validation → limits FORGE library
   Impact: Analyst Trap → zero physical artifacts → zero validation
   Elevate: Design-First Hour + first prototype deadline

4. KNOWLEDGE BASE FORMALIZATION (~20%)
   Type: Knowledge constraint
   Location: BRIDGE → limits all downstream activities
   Impact: Knowledge locked in heads → existential risk if people leave
   Elevate: bridge-knowledge-base + monthly capture sessions
```

### SKILL 5: `dmir-lever` — Leverage Point Intervention Design

**DMIR Phase:** Intervention
**Trigger:** "intervention", "lever", "tác động ở đâu?", "highest leverage", "nên làm gì trước?"

**Workflow:**

```
Input: Constraint report (from dmir-constrain) + archetype analysis
  │
  ▼
Step 1: 🤖 Map intervention options across Meadows' 12 levels
  │     ├── L12 (Parameters): "Change a number" → weakest
  │     ├── L10 (Structure): "Restructure flows"
  │     ├── L7 (Loop gain): "Strengthen/weaken specific loops"
  │     ├── L5 (Rules): "Change the rules of the system"
  │     ├── L3 (Goals): "Change what system optimizes for"
  │     ├── L2 (Paradigm): "Change how we see the system" → strongest
  │     └── For each: specific intervention, expected impact, effort, risk
  │
  ▼
Step 2: 🤖→👤 Prioritize by leverage × feasibility
  │     ├── Matrix: Leverage (L2 highest → L12 lowest) × Feasibility (easy → hard)
  │     ├── ★ "Start with highest-leverage intervention that is ACTUALLY FEASIBLE"
  │     ├── Phase 1 interventions: high-feasibility, medium-leverage (quick wins)
  │     ├── Phase 2 interventions: medium-feasibility, high-leverage (structural)
  │     └── Phase 3 interventions: low-feasibility, highest-leverage (paradigm shifts)
  │
  ▼
Step 3: 👤 Select interventions + assign to frameworks
  │     ├── "Intervention A → implement via BRIDGE skill X"
  │     ├── "Intervention B → implement via FORGE skill Y"
  │     ├── "Intervention C → implement via HELIX skill Z"
  │     └── ★ "Which framework gets the intervention?" = routing decision
  │
  ▼
Output: Intervention plan → routed to specific BRIDGE/FORGE/HELIX skills
```

**★ Key Design Choice: dmir-lever ROUTES interventions TO the triad.**
```
dmir-lever identifies: "Need to improve signal extraction rate"
  → Routes to: bridge-signal-extract (the skill that implements it)

dmir-lever identifies: "Need fallback for LOMAH-AD"
  → Routes to: forge-fallback (the skill that implements it)

dmir-lever identifies: "Need sync protocol between domains"
  → Routes to: helix-sync-protocol (the skill that implements it)

DMIR = THINK (what to do)
BRIDGE/FORGE/HELIX = DO (how to do it)
```

### SKILL 6: `dmir-reflect` — After-Action Review

**DMIR Phase:** Reflection
**Trigger:** "review", "AAR", "what did we learn?", "reflection", "rút kinh nghiệm"

**Workflow:**

```
Input: Completed intervention/project/decision + outcomes
  │
  ▼
Step 1: 👤→🤖 Structured AAR
  │     ├── What was intended? (plan, prediction, expected outcome)
  │     ├── What actually happened? (results, surprises, failures)
  │     ├── Why the gap? (root cause analysis, not blame)
  │     │   ├── 🤖 Compare predicted vs. actual using dmir-model
  │     │   ├── 👤 Add context model can't capture (politics, morale, timing)
  │     │   └── Together: "model predicted X but reality was Y because Z"
  │     ├── What will we do differently? (specific changes)
  │     └── What should we STOP doing? (equally important)
  │
  ▼
Step 2: 🤖 Update system knowledge
  │     ├── Update SD model parameters (if model was wrong, fix it)
  │     ├── Update archetype status (archetype weakened? strengthened?)
  │     ├── Update constraint analysis (constraint shifted?)
  │     └── Route learnings: → bridge-knowledge-base Layer 3
  │
  ▼
Step 3: 🤖→👤 Meta-reflection
  │     ├── "How good is our THINKING about the system?"
  │     ├── "Are we getting better at prediction? At intervention?"
  │     ├── "What blind spots keep recurring?"
  │     └── ★ "Are we reflecting or just going through motions?"
  │
  ▼
Output: AAR document + model updates + learning entries → KB Layer 3
```

**Frequency:**
- After every major decision: lightweight AAR (15 min)
- After every HELIX phase gate: medium AAR (30 min)  
- After every DMIR cycle (quarterly): full AAR (2 hours)
- After product launch: comprehensive AAR (half day)

### SKILL 7: `dmir-paradigm` — Formal Paradigm Challenge

**DMIR Phase:** Reflection (deep)
**Trigger:** "paradigm", "thách thức giả định", "tại sao chúng ta tin?", "sacred cow", "challenge assumptions"

**Workflow:**

```
Step 1: 🤖 Surface implicit beliefs
  │     ├── Scan decision log (from bridge-judgment): what assumptions underlie decisions?
  │     ├── Scan archetype analysis: what belief drives the symptomatic solution?
  │     ├── Extract: list of implicit paradigms
  │     │   Ví dụ Workshop X:
  │     │   ├── "Defense products cần hardware chuyên dụng đắt tiền"
  │     │   ├── "Một người kiêm nhiều vai trò là bình thường ở xưởng nhỏ"
  │     │   ├── "Phân tích kỹ trước khi làm = an toàn"
  │     │   ├── "CEO phải review mọi quyết định kỹ thuật"
  │     │   └── "Không có prototype vì chưa đủ phân tích"
  │     │
  │     └── Each belief: "If this belief is WRONG, what changes?"
  │
  ▼
Step 2: 👤 Challenge protocol (CEO + trusted advisors)
  │     ├── Pick 1-2 beliefs to challenge this quarter
  │     ├── For each: "What evidence would DISPROVE this belief?"
  │     ├── "What would we do differently if this belief were false?"
  │     ├── "What's the COST of holding this belief if it's wrong?"
