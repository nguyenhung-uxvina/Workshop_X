## MỞ ĐẦU: PURPOSE + USE OF THIS DOCUMENT

Document này là **operational companion** cho Strategy Document (1) và Technical Reference (2). Mục đích: giúp Workshop X leadership + team **bắt đầu execute** strategy hiệu quả.

Document này **không phải** aspirational document. Mỗi action item có owner, timeline, measurable outcome.

**Cách sử dụng:**
- Week 1-2: Leadership team đọc toàn bộ, align trên critical actions
- Week 3+: Reference document cho weekly operations reviews
- Monthly: Update progress against milestones
- Quarterly: Formal review + adjustment
- Annual: Major revision based on learning

## PHẦN I: FIRST 90 DAYS ACTION PLAN

**90 days establishing foundation**. Nếu không execute tốt 90 days này, cả strategy risk failure.

- All-hands meeting announcing strategic direction

- Role definition finalized

Workshop X passes 90-day foundation test if:

- ✅ Strategic commitment formalized + communicated
- ✅ Software Architect hire in pipeline
- ✅ UTT Product Manager onboarded

- ✅ Project management rigor established

### 1.5 Critical Risks First 90 Days

**Risk 1**: Software Architect hire delay → LCS timeline slips 3-6 months. Mitigation: backup plan với senior consultant.

**Risk 2**: Current mothership customer dispute → Follow-on orders in jeopardy. Mitigation: proactive senior relationship management.

**Risk 3**: Team not aligned with strategic direction → Execution issues. Mitigation: over-communicate, address concerns, involve team.

**Risk 4**: Budget constraints earlier than planned → Can't execute Phase 1. Mitigation: phased spending discipline, revenue urgency.

**Risk 5**: Key infrastructure delays → Team unproductive. Mitigation: procurement priority, backup options.

Key stakeholders trong VN Navy:

- Focus: Specifications, compliance, quality, costs
- Language: Technical, specification-oriented

- High decision influence on contract terms

*Training command:*
- Focus: Curriculum integration, trainer effectiveness
- Language: Pedagogical, realistic training scenarios
- Regular collaboration
- High decision influence on UTT specifications

*Strategic command:*
- Focus: Long-term capability, regional positioning
- Language: Strategic, multi-year horizon
- Annual + major decisions
- High decision influence on major programs

### 4.3 First Customer Engagement Script (UTT with Navy MCM)

**Meeting Purpose**: Introduce UTT capability, understand MCM training needs, explore collaboration.

**Pre-meeting preparation:**
- Attendees researched
- Workshop X credentials document
- UTT technical brief (bilingual)
- Success story examples
- Clear asks + offers defined
- Next steps prepared

**Meeting Flow (90 minutes):**

*Minutes 0-10: Relationship context*
- Introductions, Workshop X background
- Confirm mutual acquaintances, shared history
- Set tone: collaborative, respectful, exploratory

*Minutes 10-30: Navy current state + pain points*
- Ask about current MCM training
- Understand current targets (imports, ad-hoc)
- Pain points (cost, availability, realism, flexibility)
- Training frequency + scenarios desired
- **Listen more than talk**

*Minutes 30-60: UTT capability presentation*
- Technical capabilities (brief, relevance-focused)
- Signature fidelity demonstrated
- Variants available
- Recovery systems (reusability emphasized)
- Operational integration
- Case studies or prototype results
- **Connect specifically to pain points heard**

*Minutes 60-75: Discussion + questions*
- Navy questions
- Clarifications
- Concerns addressed
- Regulatory + procurement discussions

*Minutes 75-90: Next steps + commitments*
- Interest confirmation
- Next meeting scheduled
- Action items per side
- Documents exchanged

**Post-meeting follow-up:**
- Thank you note within 24 hours
- Meeting summary + commitments within 48 hours
- Promised materials delivered
- Next milestone tracking

## PHẦN V: DECISION GATES + REVIEW PROCESSES

### 5.1 Governance Architecture

**Annual**: Strategic review + adjustment, full strategy review, major adjustments, budget allocation

**Quarterly**: Business performance review — revenue, costs, margin, team health, customer status, risk register

**Monthly**: Operations review — financial actuals, project status, risk flags, hiring progress, near-term decisions

**Weekly**: Leadership huddle — critical issues, decisions needed, team updates, blockers

### 5.2 Decision Gates

**Gate 1 — End of G1 (M18):**

*Review criteria:*
- Financial: Revenue tracking, CapEx spend, margin
- Operational: ISO 9001 certified, team stabilized, processes mature
- Customer: Anchor customers, follow-on orders, satisfaction
- Technical: LCS v1.0 deployed, UTT production, quality
- Strategic: Market signals, competition assessment

*Decision outputs:*
- **Green**: Phase 2 approved full scope ($8-12M funding)
- **Yellow**: Phase 2 approved with adjustments
- **Red**: Fundamental rethink required

**Gate 2 — Mid Phase 2 (M30):**
- LCS v2.0 progress
- MSM-12 development
- UTT production scaling
- Export market first engagement
- AS9100D progression

**Gate 3 — End of Phase 2 (M42):**
- LCS v2.0 deployed
- MSM-12 in production
- UTT full variant line
- First export orders
- Phase 3 funding decision

**Gate 4 — End of Phase 3 (M66):**
- Full product portfolio operational
- USV launch assessed
- Revenue $50M+ run rate
- Phase 4 scope + M&A potential

### 5.3 Gate Review Process

**Preparation (4 weeks before):**
- Data gathering
- Financial reconciliation
- Customer surveys
- Technical reviews
- Risk assessments
- Recommendation preparation

**Review meeting (full day):**
- Review all data
- Discussion per area
- Issues + concerns addressed
- Decision made
- Next phase planning initiated

**Post-gate (2 weeks):**
- Communication to team
- Budget finalization
- Resource allocation updates
- Updated plan communicated

### 5.4 Continuous Improvement

**Lessons learned capture:**
- Each major milestone: post-mortem
- Sprint retrospectives (agile teams)
- Customer feedback sessions
- Competitor analysis

**Methodology updates:**
- Quarterly review of process effectiveness
- Adjustments to operating cadence
- Tool + template improvements
- Knowledge base additions
