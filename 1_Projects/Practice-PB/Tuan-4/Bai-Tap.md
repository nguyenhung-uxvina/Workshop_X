## BT-4.3: Sensitivity Analysis Practice (T4, 60 phút)

**Mô tả:** Vary top-3 criteria weights ±1 trong VDI 2225 evaluation, check robustness.

**Input:** BT-4.2 evaluation results (hoặc BT-2.1)

**Output:**
- Original ranking
- Ranking after weight variation (9 scenarios: 3 criteria × 3 variations)
- Robustness conclusion: stable / sensitive / inconclusive
- Decision confidence: high / medium / low

**Tiêu chí đạt:**
- [ ] Top-3 weights identified by impact
- [ ] ≥6 sensitivity scenarios tested
- [ ] Rank reversals documented (if any)
- [ ] Final decision justified with sensitivity evidence
- [ ] One-paragraph conclusion: "Concept X is robust/fragile because..."
