# ACH Thesis Publication --- Project Brief

## Problem Statement
Workshop X's ACH (Adaptive Capability Hypothesis) thesis needs to be articulated and published to build credibility in the defense training ecosystem. The core argument --- competing on superior adaptability rather than lower cost --- is central to Workshop X's strategy but has not been formally documented or shared.

## Objective
Produce a publishable thesis document that communicates the ACH framework, with carefully calibrated disclosure (Goldilocks Disclosure) that builds reputation without exposing competitive advantage.

## Scope

### In Scope
- ACH thesis core argument and framework
- Goldilocks Disclosure decision (what to publish vs. protect)
- Draft document suitable for target venue
- Media-Phase-Activation plan (sequential after draft)

### Out of Scope
- Classified operational details
- Specific supplier pricing or proprietary design data
- Product-level technical specifications

## Success Criteria
- [ ] Thesis draft reviewed and approved by CEO
- [ ] Goldilocks Disclosure matrix completed (Core decision)
- [ ] First public content published

## Constraints
- Solo engineer bandwidth (Tier 3 = lower priority than Tier 1/2)
- Disclosure must not compromise IRONMESH portfolio IP
- Sequential: Media activation only after thesis draft is solid

## COD Classification
- **Core**: Thesis argument, disclosure boundaries, final review
- **Offload**: Research compilation, formatting, draft sections
- **Default**: Distribution logistics
