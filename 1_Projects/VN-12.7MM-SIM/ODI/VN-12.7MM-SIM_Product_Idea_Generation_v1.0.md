**Customer:** Vietnamese Navy patrol boat 12.7mm gunner crews (~600 gunners)
**Budget:** ≤$45,000/unit | **Timeline:** 12 months to prototype

---

## 3-DIMENSION IDEA MATRIX

**DIMENSION 1 — FUNCTION:**
- F-a) Basic gunnery skill training (replicate live-fire, new delivery)
- F-b) Extended: gunnery + tactical judgment + crew coordination
- F-c) New function: AI-coached adaptive training with predictive readiness scoring

**DIMENSION 2 — WORKING PRINCIPLE:**
- W-a) Physical replica + visual display screens (conventional simulator)
- W-b) VR/AR headset-based (no physical displays)
- W-c) Mixed reality: physical mount + projected/AR environment overlay

**DIMENSION 3 — EMBODIMENT:**
- E-a) Full-size fixed installation (training center)
- E-b) Portable/deployable (ship-transportable, fits container)
- E-c) Software-only / tablet (no physical hardware)

### Cross-Cutting Requirements (All Hardware Variants — v2.0 Addition)

| Requirement | Rationale from Competitive Analysis |
|-------------|-------------------------------------|
| **Absolute encoders** (not incremental) | Chinese products use incremental → position drift after 12–18 months. Absolute = no drift, no recalibration needed. |
| **Tropical hardening**: operating range 0–45°C, 10–95% RH non-condensing | NATO products designed for European temperate. VN climate is structural advantage if designed-in from start. |
| **UAS/drone threat library** standard | All NATO products (Saab, Rheinmetall, InVeris) acknowledge UAS library as underdeveloped. PLA drone profiles (DJI-class, CH-4/5, WZ-9) are VN-specific requirement. |
| **Round-level 3D AAR** | Saab Gamer benchmark — must match to compete in premium tier; Chinese products have only score+video. |

### I-06 Elevation Rationale (NEW v2.0)

Competitive analysis (REF-02, REF-03) confirms: **no NATO product at any price tier** offers native crew coordination training (gunner + loader + commander simultaneously). This is documented in:
- Saab Gamer: "Individual position training only — no formal crew team scoring" (REF-02 §7)
- Rheinmetall AVGTS: "Trains the individual gunner and small crew. Integration with higher-echelon C2 is limited." (REF-03 §7)
- InVeris FATS: "No crew coordination training" (REF-01 §7)

I-06 addresses ODI outcomes O08 (threat variety), O10 (repetitions), and implicitly O02 (scenario variety — crew scenarios are a new category). The naval operations context (patrol boat crew = gunner + loader + boat commander) makes crew coordination training **operationally required**, not optional. I-06 should be designed as the **Phase 2 hardware expansion** after I-01 is proven.

### Rationale for I-02 Inclusion

I-02 (Containerized) addresses a gap in the current I-01 strategy:
- I-01 assumes permanent training center (shore-based)
- Vietnamese Navy has bases across 3,200 km coastline — not all have training centers
- A containerized unit deploys by truck/ship to remote bases (Cat Ba, Phu Quoc, Truong Sa)
- Marginal cost over I-01: ~$10-15K (container fit-out, vibration isolation, HVAC)
- Serves ODI outcomes O03 (time to train) and O06 (cancellation reduction) for distributed fleet
