# VN-12.7MM-SIM-TAB — PRODUCT PROPOSAL

## 12.7mm Gunnery Tablet Trainer (Software-Only)

---

## 1. PRODUCT SUMMARY

- **Product name:** 12.7mm Gunnery Tablet Trainer
- **Codename:** HỒNG HẢI POCKET
- **Product code:** VN-12.7MM-SIM-TAB
- **Primary function:** Enable Vietnamese Navy gunner crews to practice gunnery theory, target engagement procedures, and lead calculation through a mobile application — reducing skill degradation between live-fire and simulator sessions.
- **Customer (who buys):** Vietnamese Navy Training Command — may be bundled with I-01 purchase or distributed separately as pre-training tool.
- **End-user (who uses):** 12.7mm DShK/DShKM gunner crews (~600 gunners, 50+ vessels). Also: gunnery instructors (scenario authoring), training officers (progress monitoring).
- **Target market:** Vietnamese Navy — all gunner crews with access to Android/iOS tablets or smartphones. No infrastructure required.

### Role in Product Family

```
I-09 is the COMPANION product:
  - Pre-training: gunners study theory + practice procedures BEFORE simulator session
  - Skill retention: gunners practice between I-01 sessions (addresses O19 directly)
  - Sales pipeline: demonstrate software capability to Navy before I-01 hardware delivery
  - Data seed: early performance data feeds IRONMESH analytics (validates data pipeline)
```

### Cost Target

- **Development cost:** ≤$10,000 total (4-month sprint, primarily SW developer time)
- **Per-unit cost:** ~$0 (software distribution) or ≤$500 if bundled with ruggedized tablet hardware
- **vs import equivalent:** No direct import equivalent exists. REF-05 competitive analysis confirms no western tablet-based gunnery trainer available below $10K development cost. Closest: generic military training apps at $50-200K development.
- **Revenue model options:** (a) Free — bundled with I-01 purchase, (b) License — $200-500/unit/year for standalone, (c) Freemium — basic free, advanced scenarios paid

### Delivery Target

- **MVP (v1.0):** ≤4 months from project start
- **v1.0 scope:** Theory modules + 2D engagement practice + scoring + offline capability
- **v2.0 (3-month follow-on):** 3D gyroscope aiming + scenario editor + IRONMESH sync
- **Hardware requirement:** Android 10+ tablet or phone with gyroscope. No custom hardware.

### Key Constraint

**Single most important boundary:** Perceived seriousness by military users. If the tablet app is perceived as a "game" rather than a legitimate training tool, adoption will fail regardless of educational effectiveness. This drives the requirement for military-grade UI (no gamification chrome), doctrine-aligned scenario structure, and integration with formal training records (O17, O18).

## 4. WORKING PRINCIPLE OPTIONS (3 — Phase 2 Selects)

### Option A: 2D Touch-Based Trainer

- Simplified top-down or side-view engagement display
- Touch controls: tap to fire, drag to aim, pinch to zoom
- Focus: theory, procedures, engagement sequence practice
- **Pros:** Fastest to build (2 months), works on any device, smallest file size
- **Cons:** Lowest engagement realism, no spatial reasoning practice
- **Estimated cost:** ~$5K development

### Option B: 3D Gyroscope-Based Trainer

- 3D naval scene rendered on device. Phone/tablet = aiming device (gyro input)
- User physically moves device to traverse/elevate, tap to fire
- Focus: spatial awareness, lead calculation, burst timing
- **Pros:** Physical aiming practice transfers to simulator/real weapon. Engaging.
- **Cons:** Requires gyroscope (most modern phones have one). Motion sickness risk.
- **Estimated cost:** ~$8K development

### Option C: Hybrid — Theory + 3D Engagement + AI Tutor

- Combines A (theory modules) with B (3D gyro engagement)
- Adds: AI tutor that identifies weak areas, recommends drills, tracks mastery
- Shared scoring algorithm with I-01 (enables cross-device comparison)
- **Pros:** Maximum ODI outcome coverage. Strongest I-01 companion value. IRONMESH data pipeline.
- **Cons:** Highest complexity. 4-month timeline tight for full scope. AI tutor needs data.
- **Estimated cost:** ~$10K development (v1.0 = A+B, v2.0 adds AI tutor)

**Recommendation:** Option C with staged delivery — v1.0 ships A+B (4 months), v2.0 adds AI tutor (3-month follow-on). This manages timeline risk while maximizing long-term value.

## 5. RISK ASSESSMENT

### Technical: LOW

- **Top concern:** 3D rendering performance on low-end military devices. Vietnamese military tablets may be 2-3 generations old. Mitigation: target Android 10+, test on $150 devices, use low-poly assets.
- **Secondary:** Gyroscope calibration accuracy varies by device. Mitigation: calibration routine at app start, drift compensation algorithm.
- **No novel technology required.** Mobile 3D, gyro input, offline storage — all mature (TRL 7).

### Schedule: LOW

- **Top concern:** Simulation developer shared with I-01. If I-01 Phase 3 work takes priority, I-09 development may stall. Mitigation: I-09 uses simplified SW core — can be developed by a separate mobile developer with ballistic model as a library.
- **4-month timeline is achievable** for v1.0 scope (theory + 2D/3D engagement + scoring). AI tutor deferred to v2.0.

### Market: MEDIUM

- **Top concern:** Military doctrine may not recognize tablet-based training as "real training." If training officers don't assign app practice as official pre-training, adoption depends entirely on individual gunner motivation.
- **Mitigation:** Bundle with I-01 as mandatory pre-training requirement. Training officer can see completion status. Integrate into formal training plan (not optional self-study).
- **Secondary:** Data security — military performance data on personal devices. May require MDM (Mobile Device Management) or offline-only mode with manual sync.

### Kill Condition

> **If Navy Training Command explicitly rejects mobile/tablet training as doctrinally unacceptable after formal presentation, STOP standalone development.** Pivot to: embedded pre-training module within I-01 instructor station (same content, different delivery — no mobile device needed). Development effort is not wasted — 3D assets and ballistic model feed I-01 directly.
