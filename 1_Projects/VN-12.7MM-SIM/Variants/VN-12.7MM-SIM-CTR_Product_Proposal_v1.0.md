# VN-12.7MM-SIM-CTR — PRODUCT PROPOSAL

## Containerized 12.7mm Naval Gunnery Training System

---

## 1. PRODUCT SUMMARY

- **Product name:** Containerized 12.7mm Naval Gunnery Trainer
- **Product code:** VN-12.7MM-SIM-CTR (variant of VN-12.7MM-SIM-001)
- **Primary function:** Deliver shore-equivalent gunnery simulation training to any naval base or port facility using a self-contained, truck-deployable container — eliminating the requirement for a permanent training center.
- **Customer (who buys):** Vietnamese Navy Training Command — base commanders at regional facilities without permanent training centers.
- **End-user (who uses):** 12.7mm DShK/DShKM gunner crews at remote and distributed naval stations (Cat Ba, Phu Quoc, Con Dao, Truong Sa, Cam Ranh, etc.)
- **Target market:** Vietnamese Navy — 10+ naval bases across 3,200 km coastline; focus on bases that cannot justify a permanent training center but need regular gunnery qualification.

### Cost Target

- **Unit cost:** ≤$55,000 (I-01 at $45K + container fit-out ≤$10K)
- **vs import equivalent:** No direct import equivalent exists for containerized 12.7mm DShK trainer. REF-05 competitive matrix confirms no deployable variant below $200K in any competitor portfolio. Closest: Meggitt deployable trainer ~$300K+. Target ≤18% of import price.

### Delivery Target

- **First unit:** 3 months after I-01 prototype validated (month 15 from project start)
- **Dependency:** I-01 must pass acceptance testing first. CTR does NOT proceed until I-01 trainer station is proven.

### Key Constraint

**Single most important boundary:** The trainer station inside the container MUST be identical to I-01. Zero software forking. Any change to I-01 automatically applies to CTR. This is a MODULARITY constraint, not a cost constraint — if the designs diverge, maintenance becomes unsustainable.

## 4. WORKING PRINCIPLE OPTIONS (3 — Phase 2 Selects)

### Option A: Standard ISO Container

- Standard 20ft ISO shipping container (6.1 × 2.4 × 2.6m)
- I-01 trainer station bolted to container floor with vibration isolation
- Split HVAC unit (external condenser, internal evaporator)
- Fold-out side panel for instructor station workspace
- Power: external 220VAC 50Hz connection (naval base shore power)

**Pro:** Cheapest, standard logistics, fits any truck/ship.
**Con:** 2.6m internal height is tight for mount + operator (need G-010: ≥2.8m ceiling). May require high-cube container (2.9m internal).

### Option B: High-Cube Container with Expanding Section

- 20ft high-cube container (2.9m internal height)
- One hydraulic expanding side (+1.2m width when deployed) for instructor area
- Integral HVAC + UPS
- Self-leveling jacks for uneven ground

**Pro:** Solves ceiling height. Instructor gets proper workspace. More professional presentation.
**Con:** +$3-5K for expanding section. Heavier. More complex maintenance.

### Option C: Custom Shelter (Non-ISO)

- Purpose-built shelter on standard truck chassis dimensions
- Optimized internal layout for trainer + instructor + small classroom (4 seats)
- Integral generator option for bases without reliable shore power

**Pro:** Maximum flexibility. Can include mini-classroom for briefing/debrief.
**Con:** Not ISO standard — limits transport options. Higher cost. Longer lead time.

## 5. RISK ASSESSMENT

### Technical Risk: **LOW**

- Top concern: **Vibration isolation** — container transport subjects trainer to road/sea vibration. Encoders and monitors must survive transit without recalibration. Mitigation: standard anti-vibration mounts (proven in military containerized systems). Test with 100 km road transport + recalibration check.
- Ceiling height for Option A is a known constraint — mitigated by selecting high-cube (Option B).

### Schedule Risk: **LOW**

- Top concern: **I-01 dependency** — CTR cannot start integration until I-01 trainer station is validated. If I-01 delays, CTR delays proportionally. Mitigation: container fit-out design can proceed in parallel with I-01 prototype build.

### Market Risk: **LOW-MEDIUM**

- Top concern: **Demand uncertainty for deployable variant** — Navy may prefer to build permanent training centers rather than deploy containers. Mitigation: container is reversible decision — if demand is low, container can be repurposed. CTR investment is incremental ($10K over I-01), not standalone.
- Secondary: some remote bases may lack adequate shore power. Option C (integral generator) mitigates but adds cost.

### Kill Condition

> **If I-01 (Standard Fixed Trainer) fails acceptance testing, CTR is automatically killed.** CTR has zero standalone value — it is I-01 in a box. If the core product fails, the container variant fails.
