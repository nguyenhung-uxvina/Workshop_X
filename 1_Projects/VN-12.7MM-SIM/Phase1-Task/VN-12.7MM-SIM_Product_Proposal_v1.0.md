# VN-12.7MM-SIM — PRODUCT PROPOSAL

## 12.7mm Naval Mount Gunnery Training Simulator (Standard Fixed)

---

## 1. PRODUCT SUMMARY

- **Product name:** 12.7mm Naval Mount Gunnery Training Simulator
- **Codename:** HỒNG HẢI (Red Sea)
- **Product code:** VN-12.7MM-SIM-001
- **Primary function:** Enable Vietnamese Navy gunner crews to develop and maintain 12.7mm gunnery proficiency through realistic simulated engagements — without consuming ammunition, requiring ship availability, or depending on weather conditions.
- **Customer (who buys):** Vietnamese Navy Training Command — procurement driven by Navy Training Modernization Program directive.
- **End-user (who uses):** 12.7mm DShK/DShKM gunner crews on patrol boats (TT-400TP, Project 10412, various PB classes) — approximately 600 gunners across 50+ vessels with 100+ mount positions.
- **Target market:** Vietnamese Navy — shore-based training centers at major naval bases (6–8 facilities initially, 10–20 trainer units).

### Cost Target

- **Unit production cost:** ≤$45,000 (CO-001)
- **vs NATO import:** ≤8% of comparable crew-served station ($500K–$1.5M) — confirmed REF-01/02/03
- **vs Chinese entry-level:** Equivalent price ($30–60K) but superior fidelity — absolute encoders (no drift), round-level AAR, published r² vs score-only
- **Payback:** ≤7.4 months based on $562K/year savings vs live-fire (charter §7.3)

### Delivery Target

- **Prototype:** ≤12 months from project start (SC-001)
- **Production ready:** ≤18 months (SC-002)

### Key Constraint

**Scoring validity.** If simulator scores do not predict live-fire qualification results (r² < 0.5), the product becomes an expensive video game — not a training tool. Drives ballistic model fidelity (BM-005: 6-DOF) and control feel realism (K-010: ±20% of real weapon) as non-negotiable MUST requirements. Kill condition is directly tied to this constraint.

## 4. WORKING PRINCIPLE OPTIONS (Phase 2 Selects)

### Option A: Budget Trainer (V1)

- Simplified mount, desktop monitors, basic controls — trigger + basic traverse/elevation, no force feedback
- Lower fidelity: fails ODI O07 (control feel). Insufficient for qualification training.
- **Cost:** ~$20–25K | **VDI 2225:** 0.64 normalised

### Option B: Standard Trainer (V2) ← SELECTED

- Full DShK replica mount, adjustable magnetic brake resistance (6-DOF fidelity), 3× curved monitors (90°+ FOV)
- Instructor station: scenario control + round-level 3D AAR replay
- **Cost:** ~$35–45K | **VDI 2225:** 0.75 normalised
- **Selected rationale:** Highest VDI 2225 score within CO-001 ($45K) budget. V3 ties score but exceeds budget. V1 fails fidelity threshold.

### Option C: Enhanced Trainer (V3)

- Full-motion platform (ship motion simulation), recoil simulation, high-end curved LED displays, full weapon weight/balance replica
- **Cost:** ~$80–120K | **VDI 2225:** 0.75 normalised — ties V2 but fails CO-001 budget constraint
- **Role:** Future premium export variant (Phase 3+) — not domestic primary product

> **Note — V4 Replica variant** assessed separately (VDI 2225: 0.71). Deferred: supply chain risk on replica components outweighs fidelity gain vs V2. Not included in Phase 2 evaluation per P11 3-option constraint.

## 5. RISK ASSESSMENT

### Technical: MEDIUM

- **Top concern:** 3D simulation software capability gap — Workshop X has no prior game engine experience. Unity/Godot developer hire or game studio partnership required within first 4 weeks of project.
- **Secondary:** Ballistic model validation — 6-DOF trajectory must match published DShK ballistic tables within 5% (BM-010). Requires comparison against live-fire data (coordinate with BMT-01 team).
- **Tertiary:** Force feedback tuning — magnetic brake resistance must feel correct to experienced gunners (K-010: ±20% of real weapon). Subjective evaluation required; tied to [ASSUMPTION] tag on PR2.

### Schedule: LOW-MEDIUM

- **Top concern:** Simulation developer availability — if hire/partner not found by month 2, software timeline slips 3–6 months. Mechanical and electronics can proceed in parallel.
- **Mitigation:** I-09 TAB, I-06 TAC crew scoring SW can overlap — shared engine means one developer benefits the entire product family.

### Market: LOW

- **Top concern (domestic):** Navy procurement cycle — even with directive, formal procurement may take 6–12 months after prototype demonstration.
- **Mitigation (domestic):** Tablet app (I-09) generates early revenue within 4–6 months. Container variant (I-02) addresses remote bases with faster procurement authority.
- **Export market:** Credible $30–80K export position confirmed (Myanmar, Bangladesh, Cambodia, Africa). Chinese products do not compete domestically (VN policy) — removes most price-aggressive competitor from home market.

### Kill Condition

> **If prototype testing shows sim-to-live correlation r² < 0.50 after full tuning cycle (3 iterations), STOP the project.** The core value proposition — simulator training improves real gunnery performance — is unproven. Pivoting to a "familiarisation trainer" (lower value, lower price) may be possible but requires a new business case.
