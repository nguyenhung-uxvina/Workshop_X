# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Section 3.1 of Pahl & Beitz establishes **Product Planning** as the critical upstream phase that determines the success or failure of all subsequent design work. This phase bridges strategic company goals with actionable design specifications, converting market stimuli into structured requirements lists that guide designers through the entire development process.

## Core Message

> **Product Planning is NOT optional pre-work—it IS the foundation that determines whether design effort creates value or waste.**

## Key Insights for Defense Systems

| Aspect | Key Learning | Defense Application |
|--------|--------------|---------------------|
| **Sources of Design Tasks** | Business-to-business orders vs. strategic planning | Defense contracts from Ministry vs. internal capability development |
| **Degrees of Novelty** | Original → Adaptive → Variant design | New weapon systems vs. modernization vs. configuration variants |
| **Product Life Cycle** | Introduction → Growth → Maturation → Saturation → Decline | Military technology obsolescence planning (5-15 year cycles) |
| **Company Goals** | Cost leadership vs. performance differentiation | Vietnamese defense: balance cost constraints with security requirements |
| **7-Step Planning Process** | Situation Analysis → Search Strategy → Product Ideas → Selection → Definition → Development | Systematic capability gap analysis and solution development |

## Why This Matters for Vietnamese Defense Industry

1. **Resource Optimization**: Limited R&D budgets require rigorous planning to maximize impact
2. **Strategic Alignment**: Products must align with national defense strategy and capability gaps
3. **Technology Transfer**: Planning enables systematic absorption of foreign technology
4. **Export Potential**: Well-planned products can serve dual domestic/export markets
5. **Risk Reduction**: Early planning catches fatal flaws before expensive development

# SKILL 1: FEYNMAN TECHNIQUE EXPLANATION

## 🎓 Product Planning - Giải Thích Đơn Giản

### Giải Thích 60 Giây

Product Planning là quá trình xác định **"CHÚNG TA NÊN LÀM GÌ?"** trước khi hỏi "chúng ta làm như thế nào?" 

Giống như một vị tướng không bao giờ ra trận mà không có kế hoạch chiến lược, kỹ sư thiết kế không bao giờ bắt đầu vẽ CAD mà không có Product Planning. Đây là giai đoạn quyết định 80% thành công của dự án với chỉ 10% ngân sách.

### 🏠 Ví Dụ Hàng Ngày

**Mở nhà hàng** (không có Product Planning):
- "Mình thích nấu phở, nên mình sẽ mở quán phở!"
- Kết quả: Phát hiện khu vực đã có 10 quán phở, không có khách

**Mở nhà hàng** (có Product Planning):
- Phân tích thị trường: Khu vực này thiếu món Nhật
- Phân tích năng lực: Mình có bạn đầu bếp Nhật
- Phân tích xu hướng: Giới trẻ thích đồ Nhật
- Kết quả: Quán sushi thành công vì đáp ứng nhu cầu thực sự

### 🎯 Ví Dụ Quốc Phòng: LOMAH System Planning

**Tình huống:** Quân đội cần hệ thống báo trượt/trúng cho trường bắn

**BẾT** (không có Product Planning):
- "Mua hệ thống LOMAH của nước ngoài về dùng!"
- Vấn đề: Không tương thích đạn Việt Nam, không có bảo trì, giá cao

**TỐT** (có Product Planning):

| Bước | Hoạt động | Kết quả |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Situation Analysis** | Khảo sát 50 trường bắn, phân tích đạn VN | Cần detect đạn 7.62mm và 12.7mm |
| **Search Strategy** | Đánh giá năng lực nội địa vs. nhập khẩu | Có thể sản xuất sensor nội địa |

### Tại Sao Product Planning Quan Trọng?

**1. Ngăn ngừa "Design Without Market"**
- 70% sản phẩm thất bại vì không đáp ứng nhu cầu thực sự
- Product Planning đảm bảo "build the right thing" trước khi "build it right"

**2. Tối ưu hóa nguồn lực**
- 80% chi phí lifecycle được quyết định trong 20% đầu tiên của dự án
- Planning sớm → ít thay đổi tốn kém sau này

**3. Giảm rủi ro chiến lược**
- Portfolio analysis cân bằng rủi ro/lợi nhuận
- Tránh đầu tư vào thị trường/công nghệ đã bão hòa

### Chunk 1: STIMULI & CONTEXT (Kích Thích & Bối Cảnh)

**Core Concept:** Product Planning không bắt đầu từ "không khí loãng" - nó được kích hoạt bởi các tín hiệu cụ thể từ thị trường, môi trường, hoặc nội bộ công ty.

**Three Stimulus Sources:**

| Source | Examples | Defense Context |
|--------|----------|-----------------|
| **Market** | Customer complaints, competitor products, market share drop | Quân đội report training ineffective, foreign systems outperform |
| **Environment** | New technology, regulations, resource changes | AI advances enable new simulators, export controls limit imports |
| **Company** | R&D breakthroughs, new production methods, rationalization | New composite capability enables lighter UAVs |

**Defense Systems Application:**

| System | Primary Stimulus | Secondary Stimulus |
|--------|------------------|-------------------|
| **AR-VR Weapon Simulator** | Technology (VR hardware advancement) | Market (training cost reduction need) |
| **12.7mm RCWS** | Market (operational requirement) | Company (existing mount expertise) |
| **Target UAV** | Environment (export restrictions on imports) | Market (training modernization) |
| **RAMS** | Technology (AI real-time analysis) | Market (marksmanship decline concerns) |
| **Training Grenade** | Market (live grenade safety issues) | Environment (safety regulations) |

### Chunk 2: SITUATION ANALYSIS (Phân Tích Tình Hình)

**Core Concept:** Before deciding WHAT to do, understand WHERE you are. Situation Analysis provides the strategic context for all subsequent planning decisions.

**Five Analysis Components:**

**1. Life Cycle Phase Recognition**
```
Introduction → Growth → Maturation → Saturation → Decline
     ↑            ↑          ↑            ↑           ↑
   High risk    Expand     Optimize     Renew or    Exit or
   High R&D     capacity   profit       diversify   replace
```

**Defense Application: Target UAV Life Cycle**
- Introduction (Year 1-2): Prototype, certification, limited deployment
- Growth (Year 3-5): Scale production, expand to more units
- Maturation (Year 6-8): Optimize costs, add variants
- Saturation (Year 9-12): Market full, competitors emerge
- Decline (Year 13+): Technology obsolete, replacement needed

**2. Product-Market Matrix**

- Technical competencies (what can we design/make?)

**4. Technology Status Determination**
- State-of-art review

### Chunk 4: IDEA GENERATION (Tạo Ý Tưởng)

**Core Concept:** New products can emerge from varying any of three dimensions: Function, Working Principle, or Embodiment. The degree of variation determines the novelty (and risk) of the product idea.

**Three-Dimensional Idea Space:**

```
                    FUNCTION
                       ↑
                       │    New Function
                       │    (highest risk/reward)
                       │
                       │    Extended Function
                       │
                       │    Complementary Function
                       │
        ──────────────┼──────────────► WORKING PRINCIPLE
              Existing │   New Physical   New Energy
              Principle│    Effect        Type
                       │
                       │
                       ▼
                   EMBODIMENT
                   (Miniaturization, Ergonomics, Aesthetics)
```

**Defense Systems Idea Generation Examples:**

| System | Function Change | Principle Change | Embodiment Change |
|--------|-----------------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Target UAV** | Add IR signature simulation | Jet → Electric propulsion | Modular payload bay |
| **LOMAH** | Add shooter identification | Acoustic → Doppler radar | Wireless transmission |
| **Weapon Simulator** | Add recoil simulation | Screen → VR headset | Portable system |
| **Training Grenade** | Add frag pattern indicator | Pyro → Electronic | Reusable design |
| **RCWS** | Add AI target tracking | Manual → Servo-electric | Modular mounting |

**Idea Generation Questions:**

**Function:**
- Which functions are required by the client?
- Which functions do we already fulfil?
- What complements existing functions?
- Which functions represent a generalisation?

**Working Principle:**
- Would a change of working principle lead to better products?
- What physical effects could replace current approach?
- What energy types could be utilized?

**Embodiment:**
- Is the space used still appropriate?
- Should we focus on miniaturisation?
- Is the shape still appealing?
- Could the ergonomics be better?

### Chunk 5: IDEA SELECTION (Chọn Lọc Ý Tưởng)

### Chunk 6: PRODUCT DEFINITION (Định Nghĩa Sản Phẩm)

**Core Concept:** Selected ideas must be elaborated into concrete Product Proposals with sufficient detail for development, but not so much detail that they constrain design creativity.

**Product Proposal Components:**

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    PRODUCT PROPOSAL                          │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                              │
│  1. INTENDED FUNCTIONS                                       │
│     • Primary function (what the product MUST do)           │
│     • Secondary functions (what it SHOULD do)               │
│     • Auxiliary functions (what it COULD do)                │
│                                                              │
│  2. PRELIMINARY REQUIREMENTS LIST                           │
│     • Using Section 5.2 characteristics                     │
│     • Demands (MUST) vs Wishes (WANT)                       │
│     • Quantified where possible                             │
│                                                              │
```

### Chunk 7: PLANNING IN PRACTICE (Thực Hành Planning)

**Core Concept:** Real-world product planning requires adapting the theoretical framework to organizational realities, resource constraints, and project-specific circumstances.

**Practical Guidelines:**

**1. Organization Scaling**

|--------------|--------------------------|

| Novelty Type | Planning Intensity | Team Structure |
|--------------|-------------------|----------------|
| Existing product line extension | Light | Existing product team |
| New product in existing market | Medium | Cross-functional team |
| Diversification (new market + new product) | Heavy | Dedicated planning group |

**3. Client Integration Approaches**
- **Lead Users:** Intensive collaboration with advanced customers
- **QFD Methods:** Systematic translation of customer voice

**4. Risk Management in Planning**
- Technical failure impact on reputation
- Delayed market entry consequences
- Need for product champion (senior sponsor)

- Appropriate for important business areas
- High effort but high value for strategic decisions
- Covers scenario preparation, field analysis, forecasts, and building

### Common Deficiencies in Defense Product Planning

**Deficiency 1: Technology Push vs. Market Pull**
```
❌ WRONG: "We have sensor technology, let's make a LOMAH"
✅ RIGHT: "Training ranges need automated scoring → sensor technology fits"
```
**Fix:** Always start from market/customer need, then match to capabilities

**Deficiency 2: Premature Solution Lock-In**
```
❌ WRONG: "LOMAH system using acoustic triangulation with 4 microphones..."
✅ RIGHT: "System detecting projectile impact location to ≤5mm accuracy..."
```
**Fix:** Rewrite all solution-specific requirements as performance requirements

**Deficiency 3: Ignoring Life Cycle Positioning**
```
❌ WRONG: Planning new target drone while existing model is in growth phase
✅ RIGHT: Plan replacement when existing model reaches saturation phase
```
**Fix:** Include life cycle phase justification in every planning document

**Deficiency 4: Incomplete Stakeholder Analysis**
```
❌ WRONG: Consider only end-user (shooter)
✅ RIGHT: Include operator, maintainer, trainer, procurement, logistics
```
**Fix:** Use stakeholder map with needs for each stakeholder group

**Deficiency 5: Missing Competition Analysis**
```
❌ WRONG: "We need a training simulator" (no reference to existing options)
✅ RIGHT: Detailed comparison with domestic + imported alternatives
```
**Fix:** Include competitor matrix in every situation analysis

### MNEMONIC 1: 7 Steps of Product Planning

**🎯 Target Concept:** The seven main steps of the Product Planning process from Figure 3.2

**🧠 Primary Mnemonic:**
**Type:** Story + Vietnamese Acronym
**Mnemonic:** **"PHÂN TÍCH CHIẾN LƯỢC TÌM CHỌN ĐỊNH SẢN PHẨM"** (P-T-C-T-C-Đ)

Or simplified: **"PTCTCĐ"** = **"Phân Tích Con Tàu Cảng Định"**

*Imagine analyzing (Phân Tích) a ship (Con Tàu) at the harbor (Cảng) to determine (Định) if it's the right product*

**📖 Component Breakdown:**
- **P** = Phân tích tình hình (Analyse Situation)
- **T** = Tìm chiến lược (Formulate Strategies)
- **C** = Chế tạo ý tưởng (Find Product Ideas)
- **T** = Tuyển chọn (Select Ideas)
- **C** = Chi tiết hóa sản phẩm (Define Products)
- **Đ** = Đề xuất (Product Proposal)

**💡 Memory Reinforcement:**
Visualize a harbor scene: You ANALYZE the ships, STRATEGIZE which to load, FIND the best cargo, SELECT the items, DEFINE the manifest, then submit the shipping PROPOSAL.

**✅ Quick Recall Test:**
1. What step comes after "Formulate Strategies"?
2. Name all 6 steps in order starting with P.

**🔗 Application Context:**
Use when starting ANY new product development project - these 6 steps ensure you've systematically planned before designing.

### MNEMONIC 4: Decision Criteria Weights

**🎯 Target Concept:** The three categories of decision criteria and their minimum weights (Company Goals ≥50%, Company Strengths ≥30%, Market ≥20%)

**🧠 Primary Mnemonic:**
**Type:** Number Pattern
**Mnemonic:** **"5-3-2"** = **"NĂM-BA-HAI"**

Think: "Company Goals are NUMBER ONE (5), Strengths are NUMBER TWO (3), Market is NUMBER THREE (2)"

Alternative: **"MỤC TIÊU 50, NĂNG LỰC 30, THỊ TRƯỜNG 20"**

**📖 Component Breakdown:**
- **5** = Company Goals ≥50% weight
- **3** = Company Strengths ≥30% weight
- **2** = Market factors ≥20% weight

**💡 Memory Reinforcement:**
Remember: What you WANT (goals=5) matters most, what you CAN (strengths=3) matters second, what's OUTSIDE (market=2) matters third. Internal factors always outweigh external in selection!

---

### MNEMONIC 5: Solution-Neutral Formulation

**🎯 Target Concept:** The principle that Product Proposals should describe WHAT is needed, not HOW to achieve it

**🧠 Primary Mnemonic:**
**Type:** Contrast Phrase
**Mnemonic:** **"CÁI GÌ KHÔNG PHẢI CÁCH NÀO"** (What, Not How)

Or: **"GÌ-KHÔNG-NÀO"** = **"G-K-N"**

**📖 Component Breakdown:**
- **GÌ** = What is required (performance, function)
- **KHÔNG** = NOT (prohibition)
- **NÀO** = How it works (solution, principle)

**💡 Memory Reinforcement:**
When writing requirements, always ask: "Am I describing WHAT I need, or am I describing HOW to achieve it?" If HOW, rewrite!

**✅ Quick Recall Test:**
Which is solution-neutral?
A) "System uses acoustic sensors to detect impacts"
B) "System detects impact location with ≤5mm accuracy"

Answer: B (describes WHAT accuracy, not HOW to detect)

### MNEMONIC 6: Product-Market Matrix Quadrants

**🎯 Target Concept:** The four quadrants of the Product-Market Matrix (Figure 3.3)

**🧠 Primary Mnemonic:**
**Type:** Visual + Story
**Mnemonic:** **"XUYÊN-MỞ-PHÁT-ĐA"**

```
                    EXISTING MARKETS    NEW MARKETS
EXISTING PRODUCTS   │ XUYÊN (Penetrate) │ MỞ (Open)     │
NEW PRODUCTS        │ PHÁT (Develop)    │ ĐA (Diversify)│
```

**📖 Component Breakdown:**
- **XUYÊN** = Field I: Market Penetration (existing products, existing markets)
- **MỞ** = Field II: Market Development (existing products, new markets)
- **PHÁT** = Field III: Product Development (new products, existing markets)
- **ĐA** = Field IV: Diversification (new products, new markets) - HIGHEST RISK

**💡 Memory Reinforcement:**
Start from safest (XUYÊN - drill into what you know) → move to riskiest (ĐA - diverse into unknown). Risk increases as you move away from what you know!

### MNEMONIC 7: Idea Generation Dimensions

**🎯 Target Concept:** The three dimensions for generating product ideas (Function, Working Principle, Embodiment)

**🧠 Primary Mnemonic:**
**Type:** Acronym
**Mnemonic:** **"FUN-WORK-BODY"** = **"CHỨC-NGUYÊN-HÌNH"**

Or Vietnamese: **"CNH"** = **"Công Nghiệp Hóa"** (easy to remember phrase)

**📖 Component Breakdown:**
- **C(hức năng)** = Function: What does it DO?
- **N(guyên lý)** = Working Principle: HOW does it work?
- **H(ình dạng)** = Embodiment: What FORM does it take?

**💡 Memory Reinforcement:**
Every product idea can be varied along these three dimensions. Changing FUNCTION is most novel, changing EMBODIMENT is least novel.

```
Delay: 1-2 days in brainstorming sessions
```
