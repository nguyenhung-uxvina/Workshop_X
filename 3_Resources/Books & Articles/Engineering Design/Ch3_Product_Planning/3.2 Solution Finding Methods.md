## Executive Summary

Section 3.2 "Solution Finding Methods" is the **methodological engine** of Pahl & Beitz systematic design. While function structures define WHAT needs to be solved, this section teaches HOW to find solutions systematically—eliminating reliance on random inspiration and enabling reproducible innovation.

**Core Insight:** Solutions are not "discovered" randomly; they can be **systematically elaborated** using three complementary method families:
1. **Conventional Methods** (L→R: Literature, Analogies, Analysis)
2. **Intuitive Methods** (I→D: Intuition stimulated through group dynamics)
3. **Discursive Methods** (D→S: Deliberate step-by-step search)

**Vietnamese Mnemonic:** "**LÝ-TRỰC-GIÁC**" (Lý luận - Trực giác - Giác quan) = Logic - Intuition - Systematic Search

## Skill 1: Feynman Technique
### Core Concept Explanation - Solution Finding Methods

### 💡 60-Second Explanation

**Solution Finding Methods** are systematic ways to generate design alternatives without waiting for random inspiration. Instead of hoping a "eureka moment" arrives, engineers use structured techniques to methodically explore the solution space.

Think of it like cooking: a chef doesn't randomly throw ingredients together hoping for a good dish. They use **recipes** (conventional methods), **creative experimentation** (intuitive methods), and **systematic ingredient pairing** (discursive methods).

### 🏠 Everyday Analogy

Imagine you're moving to a new apartment and need furniture. You could:
1. **Conventional:** Browse IKEA catalog, visit stores, copy neighbor's setup
2. **Intuitive:** Brainstorm with friends what would be cool, get wild ideas
3. **Discursive:** List all functions (sit, sleep, store), systematically match furniture types

Solution finding methods work the same way—multiple complementary approaches ensure you don't miss good options.

### 🎯 Defense Application Examples

| Method Category | Defense/Security Application |
|:----------------|:----------------------------|
| **Literature Search** | MIL-HDBK, competitor product teardowns, patent databases |
| **Natural Systems Analysis** | Biomimetic camouflage, bird flight → UAV aerodynamics |

### ⚠️ Common Misconceptions

| ❌ Misconception | ✅ Correct Understanding |
|:-----------------|:------------------------|
| "Brainstorming means free-for-all discussion" | Brainstorming has strict rules: no criticism, 5-15 people, 30-45 min max, mix of experts and non-experts |
| "More methods = better results" | Methods must be selected for context; using all methods wastes time |
| "Discursive methods kill creativity" | Discursive methods COMPLEMENT intuition; they ensure systematic coverage |
| "Physical effects catalogs are only for physics experts" | Catalogs are lookup tools—you don't need deep physics to use them |
| "Morphological matrix always works" | Matrix requires compatible subsolutions; many combinations are physically impossible |

### 🔍 Understanding Check

**Scenario:** You're designing a Training Grenade simulator. You've exhausted obvious ideas (LED flash, speaker for bang). What solution finding method combination would you apply next?

<details>
<summary>Click for Model Answer</summary>

**Recommended Approach:**

1. **First: Analysis of Natural Systems (Bionics)**
   - Study how nature creates sudden flash/bang effects
   - Lightning, fireflies, pistol shrimp snap → new physical effects

2. **Second: Brainstorming with Mixed Group**
   - Include non-military personnel (film pyrotechnics, gaming)
   - Focus on "What other sensory cues indicate explosion?"
   - May reveal: pressure wave simulation, ground vibration, smoke

3. **Third: Systematic Physical Effects Search**
   - Use Roth catalog for "convert electrical → optical + acoustic"
   - Discover: piezoelectric flash, thermoacoustic effect, plasma discharge

4. **Fourth: Morphological Matrix**
   - Rows: Flash generation, Bang generation, Smoke generation, Timing
   - Columns: Different physical effects for each
   - Identify compatible combinations

**Key Insight:** The sequence matters—start broad (bionics, brainstorming), then narrow (catalogs, matrix).

### Memory Aids for Solution Finding Methods

### Master Mnemonic: "LÍT-ĐỪNG-MỜI" (Lít Đừng Mời)

**L**iterature → **Í**t existing → **T**ự nhiên (natural)
**Đ**ộng não → **Ừ**ng Method 635 → **N**hóm Gallery → **G**alerie Synectics
**M**ỏi vật lý → **Ờ**ther catalogs → **I**ntegration (morphological)

### Method Family Mnemonics

#### Conventional Methods: "**LÃNH ĐẠO MỚI**"
- **L**iterature search (Tra cứu tài liệu)
- **Â** Analysis - natural systems (Phân tích tự nhiên)
- **N**ghiên cứu - existing tech (Hệ thống có sẵn)
- **H**ình dung - analogies (Tương tự)
- **Đ**o đạc - measurements (Thử nghiệm)
- **Ạ** (silent - marks end)
- **O** (opening for intuitive)

**Memory story:** "Lãnh đạo mới" - New leaders first look at existing information before innovating.

#### Intuitive Methods: "**ĐỘNG NÃO CÓ 5 CÁCH**"
1. **Đ**ynamic brainstorming (Động não nhóm)
2. **N**ăm-ba-sáu Method 635 (5+3=6 people, rounds)
3. **C**allery method (Phương pháp gallery - C for display)
4. **D**elphi (Chuyên gia)
5. **S**ynectics (Tương tự phi kỹ thuật)

**Rule reminder:** "**KHÔNG CHÊ - GHI LẠI - PHÁT TRIỂN**"
- KHÔNG CHÊ: No criticism
- GHI LẠI: Record everything
- PHÁT TRIỂN: Build on others' ideas

#### Discursive Methods: "**VẬT LÝ → BẢNG → KẾT HỢP**"
- **VẬT LÝ**: Physical process study (equations)
- **BẢNG**: Classification schemes (tables)
- **KẾT HỢP**: Morphological combination

**Memory image:** Physics equations → organize in tables → combine systematically

### Brainstorming Rules Mnemonic: "**5-15-30-NÓI**"

- **5**: Minimum 5 people
- **15**: Maximum 15 people
- **30**: Maximum 30-45 minutes
- **NÓI** (speak):
  - **N**o criticism
  - **Ó** (sound of "ồ!") = Build on ideas
  - **I**nclude non-experts

### Morphological Matrix Formula

**"Mỗi hàng một chức năng, mỗi cột một giải pháp"**
(Each row one function, each column one solution)

**Combination count:** N = m₁ × m₂ × ... × mₙ
**Remember:** "**Nhân liên tục = Bùng nổ!**" (Continuous multiplication = Explosion!)

### Design Catalog Structure: "**TIÊU-GIẢI-TÍNH-GHI**"

- **TIÊU** chuẩn: Classifying criteria
- **GIẢI** pháp: Solutions column
- **TÍNH** năng: Solution characteristics
- **GHI** chú: Remarks

### Compatibility Check Mnemonic: "**E-M-S = Energy-Material-Signal**"

When verifying compatibility:
1. **E**nergy flows match?
2. **M**aterial flows match?
3. **S**ignal flows match?

If all three = ✓ Compatible
If any mismatch = ✗ Incompatible

#### Template A: Literature Search Session (90 minutes)

```
LITERATURE SEARCH FOCUS SESSION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PREPARATION (5 min before start):
□ Define search keywords (3-5 terms)
□ List target databases/sources
□ Prepare note-taking template
□ Silence notifications

PHASE 1: BROAD SEARCH (25 min)
├── Pomodoro 1 (25 min): 
│   • Search academic databases (Google Scholar, IEEE)
│   • Scan abstracts only - no deep reading
│   • Tag promising papers
│   • Target: 20+ sources identified
│
├── BREAK (5 min): Walk, stretch, no screens

PHASE 2: DEFENSE-SPECIFIC SEARCH (25 min)
├── Pomodoro 2 (25 min):
│   • Search MIL-HDBK, STANAG databases
│   • Review defense contractor publications
│   • Scan patent databases
│   • Target: 10+ defense-specific sources
│
├── BREAK (5 min): Hydrate, quick notes review

PHASE 3: SYNTHESIS (25 min)
├── Pomodoro 3 (25 min):
│   • Group sources by theme
│   • Identify key physical principles mentioned
│   • Note gaps in literature
│   • Create summary table
│   • Target: 1-page synthesis document

CLOSE-OUT (5 min):
□ Save all bookmarks/downloads organized
□ Update search log
□ Identify next search direction
□ Schedule follow-up deep reading
```

#### Template B: Brainstorming Session (45 minutes)

```
BRAINSTORMING FOCUS SESSION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PREPARATION (10 min before, facilitator only):
□ Define problem statement (1 sentence, on whiteboard)
□ Prepare recording method (flip chart, digital)
□ Verify participant list (5-15, diverse)
□ Prepare "seed" ideas if needed
□ Review 4 rules poster visible

OPENING (5 min):
├── State problem clearly
├── Review rules: No criticism, build on ideas, record all, wild welcome
├── Warm-up: "What's the most unusual X you've seen?"

PHASE 1: INITIAL BURST (15 min)
├── Free-form idea generation
├── Facilitator captures ALL ideas
├── No discussion, just generation
├── Aim: 30+ ideas
├── When slowing: "What else?" "From another field?"

PHASE 2: BUILDING (10 min)
├── "Pick an idea and extend it"
├── "Combine two ideas"
├── "What's the opposite?"
├── Facilitator notes connections

PHASE 3: WILD CARDS (10 min)
├── "What would nature do?"
├── "What would a child suggest?"
├── "What's the lazy solution?"
├── "What's the expensive solution?"

CLOSE (5 min):
├── Count total ideas
├── Quick categorization (obvious, promising, wild)
├── Thank participants
├── Schedule evaluation session (separate!)

POST-SESSION (15 min, facilitator):
□ Photograph/digitize all outputs
□ Create clean idea list
□ Preliminary grouping
□ Identify follow-up needs
```

#### Template C: Morphological Matrix Construction (120 minutes)

```
MORPHOLOGICAL MATRIX FOCUS SESSION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PREPARATION (10 min before):
□ Function structure ready (from previous work)
□ Physical effects catalogs accessible
□ Large paper/whiteboard for matrix
□ Previous brainstorm ideas available

PHASE 1: SUBFUNCTION SELECTION (20 min)
├── Pomodoro 1 (20 min):
│   • List all subfunctions from function structure
│   • Select 5-7 MAIN subfunctions for matrix rows
│   • Verify subfunctions are at same abstraction level
│   • Document selection rationale
│
├── BREAK (5 min)

PHASE 2: SOLUTION SEARCH - ROW BY ROW (50 min)
├── Pomodoro 2 (25 min):
│   • Subfunction 1: Find 3-5 solution principles
│   • Subfunction 2: Find 3-5 solution principles
│   • Use catalogs, brainstorm notes, literature
│
├── BREAK (5 min)
│
├── Pomodoro 3 (25 min):
│   • Subfunctions 3-4: Find 3-5 solution principles each
│   • Sketch each principle (not words only!)
│   • Note physical effect used
│
├── BREAK (5 min)

PHASE 3: COMPLETION & INITIAL SCAN (30 min)
├── Pomodoro 4 (25 min):
│   • Complete remaining subfunctions
│   • Review matrix completeness
│   • Identify obvious gaps
│   • Calculate N = m₁ × m₂ × ... × mₙ

PHASE 4: COMPATIBILITY SCREENING (15 min)
├── Scan for obvious incompatibilities
├── Mark promising paths through matrix
├── Identify 3-5 combinations for evaluation
├── Note compatibility concerns

CLOSE-OUT (5 min):
□ Photograph matrix
□ Document combination rationales
□ List open questions
□ Schedule evaluation session
```

**Instructions:** Given the scenario, identify the best 2-3 solution finding methods and justify your choice in under 10 minutes.

#### Drill 1.1: Time-Critical Novel Design
**Scenario:** You have 2 weeks to develop concept for Training Grenade that produces realistic flash, bang, and smoke without pyrotechnics. No existing product meets requirements. Team of 4 engineers available.

<details>
<summary>Model Answer</summary>

**Selected Methods:**
1. **Brainstorming** (1 session, 45 min) - Need novel ideas quickly, small team can participate

3. **Morphological Matrix** (2 hours) - Systematically combine flash/bang/smoke solutions

**Rejected Methods:**
- Delphi: Too slow for 2-week timeline
- Existing systems: No product meets requirements (stated)
- Physical process study: Insufficient time for deep equation analysis

**Justification:** Time constraint demands high-throughput methods. Brainstorming + catalog + matrix provides systematic coverage without extended timeline.

**Task:** Find physical effects for generating controlled infrared radiation.

<details>
<summary>Model Answer</summary>

| Physical Effect | Principle | Control Method | Pros | Cons |
|:----------------|:----------|:---------------|:-----|:-----|

### Drill Set 5: Compatibility Verification (30 minutes)

**Instructions:** Given two solution principles, verify E-M-S compatibility.

#### Drill 5.1: Target USV Power + Propulsion

**Verify Compatibility:**

<details>
<summary>Model Answer</summary>

| Flow Type | Compatibility Check | Result |
|:----------|:--------------------|:-------|

**Compatibility Verdict:** CONDITIONALLY COMPATIBLE

</details>
