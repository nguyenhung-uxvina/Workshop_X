## 1. Executive Summary

### 1.1 Project Context

The Transport UAV (Unmanned Aerial Vehicle for Cargo Transport - UAV Vận Tải) is an autonomous aerial platform designed to deliver payloads ranging from 5-50 kg over distances of 20-100 km. Unlike surveillance drones optimized for endurance, or target drones optimized for speed, Transport UAVs must balance **payload capacity** with **range** while maintaining safe, reliable autonomous operation.

**Key Difference from Previous Systems**:

| Aspect | Mixing Tap | UAV Catapult | Tethered Drone | Training Grenade | Target USV | **Transport UAV** |
|--------|------------|--------------|----------------|------------------|------------|-------------------|
| **Main Flow** | Material (water) | Energy (stored) | Energy + Signal | Energy (delayed) | Energy + Signal | **MATERIAL (cargo)** |
| **Independence** | V̇ ↔ ϑ | v ↔ θ | P ↔ h | R ↔ S | v ↔ σ | **m (payload) ↔ R (range)** |
| **Domain** | Plumbing | Mechanical | Electrical | Pyrotechnic | Marine + RF | **Aerospace + Logistics** |
| **Unique Challenge** | Pressure coupling | Gravity on rail | Tether weight | Arming sequence | Sea state | **Variable CG, wind** |
| **Environment** | Indoor | Outdoor static | Outdoor tethered | Thrown | Open sea | **3D airspace, weather** |

### 1.2 Design Objectives Summary

| Objective | Specification | Priority |
|-----------|---------------|----------|
| Payload capacity | 10-30 kg (scalable to 50 kg) | **DEMAND** |
| Range | ≥50 km one-way, 100 km round-trip | **DEMAND** |
| Cruise speed | 60-80 km/h | **DEMAND** |
| Wind tolerance | Operable in ≤10 m/s (20 knots) | **DEMAND** |
| Autonomous operation | Waypoint navigation + obstacle avoidance | **DEMAND** |
| VTOL capability | No runway required | **DEMAND** |
| Payload flexibility | Medical, ammunition, general supply | **WISH** |
| Night/all-weather | Basic IFR capability | **WISH** |
| Indigenous content | >50% local manufacturing | **WISH** |
| Unit cost | <$80,000 USD | **WISH** |

### 1.3 Key Results Preview

Following the complete 8-step process:
- **Function Structure Selected**: Modular payload bay with independent propulsion and navigation
- **Configuration Selected**: Hybrid Quadplane (VTOL + Fixed-Wing cruise)
- **Winning Solution Variant**: "Hybrid Quadplane + Modular Cargo Pod" (Variant B)
- **VDI 2225 Score**: 0.86 (highest among 4 variants)
- **Next Steps**: Aerodynamic optimization and flight control development

## 3. Step 2: Abstracting to Identify Essential Problems

### 3.1 Problem Formulation

From the requirements list, the essential problem can be stated:

> **Transport material payload (cargo) from point A to point B through the air, with VTOL capability for runway-independent operation, achieving independent adjustment of payload mass (m) and range (R), while navigating autonomously in three-dimensional space with wind and terrain considerations.**

### 3.3 Physical Relationships

**Fundamental Tradeoff - The m-R Independence Problem**:

For any aircraft, the range equation (Breguet for electric) shows:
```
R = η × (E/g) × (L/D) × (1/m_battery) × ln(m_total/m_empty)

Where:
η = propulsion efficiency (~0.7-0.85 for electric)
E = battery energy density (Wh/kg) - typically 200-250 Wh/kg
L/D = lift-to-drag ratio (8-15 for small UAV)
g = gravity

For a fixed aircraft:
- More payload mass → Less range (direct tradeoff)
- More battery → More range BUT heavier (diminishing returns)
```

**Energy Budget Analysis**:
```
For 60 kg MTOW UAV with 20 kg payload at 50 km range:

Power requirement (cruise):
P_cruise ≈ (m × g × v) / (η × L/D)
P_cruise = (60 × 9.8 × 22) / (0.8 × 12) = 1,347 W ≈ 1.4 kW

For 60 min endurance:
E_required = 1.4 kW × 1 hr = 1.4 kWh

With 80% usable capacity and 20% reserve:
E_battery = 1.4 / 0.64 = 2.2 kWh minimum
At 200 Wh/kg: m_battery = 11 kg

Mass budget:
Empty: 25 kg (airframe, motors, avionics)
Battery: 11 kg
Payload: 20 kg
Reserve: 4 kg
TOTAL: 60 kg ✓
```

### 3.4 Independence Requirements

**Critical Insight**: The Transport UAV requires:

| Variable | Symbol | Requirement |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Payload mass | m | 0-30 kg, mission-dependent |
| Range | R | 20-100 km, mission-dependent |
| Cruise speed | v | 60-80 km/h, adjustable |
| Altitude | h | 50-500 m AGL, terrain-following |

**The m-R Independence Problem**:

1. **More Payload** typically means:
   - More energy consumed per km
   - Reduced range (unless battery also increased)
   - Different CG (affects stability)
   
2. **More Range** typically requires:
   - Larger battery (heavier)
   - Less payload capacity (fixed MTOW)
   - More efficient aerodynamics

3. **Design Challenge**: Achieve useful payload AND range without one severely limiting the other

**Independence Solution Concepts**:
- **Modular battery**: Add battery packs for range at expense of payload
- **Hybrid configuration**: Efficient cruise (fixed-wing) + VTOL (rotors)
- **Optimized aerodynamics**: High L/D reduces power requirement
- **Mission planning**: Pre-compute m-R tradeoff, operator selects point

### 3.5 Configuration Alternatives

| Configuration | VTOL? | Efficiency | Complexity | Payload | Range | Decision |
|---------------|-------|------------|------------|---------|-------|----------|
| Multirotor | Yes | Low | Low | Good | **Poor** | Range insufficient |
| Fixed-wing | No | High | Low | Good | **Excellent** | Needs runway |
| Tilt-rotor | Yes | Medium | High | Medium | Good | Complex mechanism |
| **Quadplane** | **Yes** | **High cruise** | **Medium** | **Good** | **Good** | **Best balance** |
| Tail-sitter | Yes | Medium | Medium | Medium | Medium | Payload access hard |

**Justified Selection**:
> Quadplane (hybrid) configuration selected because it provides VTOL capability while achieving efficient fixed-wing cruise, balancing the m-R tradeoff better than pure multirotors.

## 4. Step 3: Establishing Function Structures

### 4.1 Subfunction Identification

The overall function decomposes into:
- **Lift/Thrust**: Generate vertical and forward force
- **Store Energy**: Contain battery/fuel for mission
- **Carry Payload**: Secure and transport cargo
- **Navigate**: Determine position and follow path
- **Control**: Execute flight commands
- **Communicate**: Data link with ground
- **Land Safely**: Precision landing at destination

## 6. Step 5: Selecting Working Principles

### 6.1 Working Principle Analysis for VTOL

**Quad-Rotor Configuration**:
```
Configuration: 4× motors in X-configuration
Motor size: 5008 (50mm stator diameter, 380 KV)
Propeller: 18" × 6.1" carbon fiber
Thrust per motor: ~8 kg static, 32 kg total
Thrust-to-weight ratio: 32/60 = 0.53 (hover) or 1.6 (max)
Disk loading: 25-35 kg/m²
Hover efficiency: ~60%
Cost: ~$400 per motor/ESC/prop set × 4 = $1,600
```

**Hex-Rotor Configuration**:
```
Configuration: 6× motors in flat hex
Motor size: 4108 (41mm stator, 380 KV)
Propeller: 15" × 5" carbon fiber
Thrust per motor: ~5 kg static, 30 kg total
Thrust-to-weight ratio: 30/60 = 0.5 (hover) or 1.5 (max)
Redundancy: Can lose 1 motor, maintain control
Hover efficiency: ~55% (smaller props less efficient)
Cost: ~$300 per set × 6 = $1,800
```

**Decision**: Quad-rotor selected for initial variant due to simplicity; hex-rotor as upgrade option for redundancy-critical missions.

### 6.2 Working Principle Analysis for Wing

**Conventional High-Wing**:
```
Wingspan: 3.5 m
Wing area: 0.7 m² (aspect ratio 17.5)
Airfoil: E214 (high lift, moderate Re)
L/D ratio: ~14 at cruise
Tail: Conventional V-tail
CG range: 25-35% MAC
Manufacturing: Carbon fiber skin, foam core
Cost: ~$3,000 for wing + tail
```

**Flying Wing**:
```
Wingspan: 3.0 m
Wing area: 0.8 m² (aspect ratio 11.25)
Airfoil: MH-45 (reflexed for stability)
L/D ratio: ~12 at cruise
Tail: Winglets for yaw control
CG range: Very tight (2-5% MAC)
Manufacturing: Complex compound curves
Cost: ~$4,000 for wing
```

**Decision**: Conventional high-wing selected for CG tolerance, easier payload integration, and simpler manufacturing.

### 6.3 Working Principle Analysis for Power

**Li-Ion (21700 cells)**:
```

Weight: 7.5 kg per pack

Swappable: Yes, quick-release mount
```

```
Weight: 8.5 kg total

Cycle life: 200-300 cycles

Swappable: Yes
```

**Decision**: Li-Ion 21700 selected for better energy density and cycle life, despite slightly higher cost.

## 10. Skill 1: Feynman Technique Analysis

### 10.1 Concept: "Hybrid VTOL Transport"

#### 60-Second Explanation (ELI12)

A Transport UAV is like a flying delivery truck that doesn't need roads OR runways!

Here's the clever part:
1. **It has TWO ways to fly**:
   - **Helicopter mode**: 4 propellers pointing UP for takeoff and landing (like a big drone)
   - **Airplane mode**: Wings for flying forward efficiently (like a paper airplane)
   
2. **Why both?** Think about it:
   - Helicopters can land anywhere but use TONS of fuel
   - Airplanes are efficient but need a runway
   - Our drone: Best of both! Helicopter for 2 minutes (takeoff/landing), airplane for 1 hour (cruise)

3. **The "Transition"**: The magic moment when it switches from hovering to flying forward. The wing starts carrying the weight, and the up-pointing propellers STOP.

It's like a car that can also become a boat - each mode is best for different situations!

### 10.2 Concept: "m-R Independence"

#### 60-Second Explanation

The tricky part: How do you carry MORE cargo WITHOUT flying SHORTER distances?

**The Problem (The Physics)**:
- Heavier load = more energy to lift = shorter range
- This is like: More passengers in your car = worse gas mileage

**The Solution (The Engineering)**:
1. **Efficient cruise**: Use WINGS not rotors for forward flight (4× less energy!)
2. **Modular batteries**: Trade cargo for range if needed
3. **Smart mission planning**: Know exactly how far you can go with each load

So instead of being "stuck" with one cargo/range combo, operators can CHOOSE:
- Heavy load, short mission: 30 kg, 30 km
- Light load, long mission: 10 kg, 80 km
- Typical mission: 20 kg, 60 km

The WING is the secret - it lets us stretch the battery further!

## 21. Skill 12: Targeted Drill Exercises

### 21.1 Drill Set A: Range Calculations

**Drill A1**: Calculate range for 50 kg aircraft with 2 kWh battery at 72 km/h cruise.

_Model Answer_:
```
Given:
m = 50 kg, E = 2 kWh, v = 72 km/h = 20 m/s
Assume L/D = 12, η = 0.75 (propulsion efficiency)

Cruise power:
P = (m × g × v) / (η × L/D)
P = (50 × 9.8 × 20) / (0.75 × 12)
P = 9800 / 9 = 1089 W ≈ 1.1 kW

Usable energy (80% of battery):
E_usable = 2 × 0.8 = 1.6 kWh

Flight time:
t = E_usable / P = 1.6 / 1.1 = 1.45 hours = 87 min

Range:
R = v × t = 72 × 1.45 = 104 km ✓
```

**Drill A2**: How does range change if payload increases from 15 kg to 25 kg?

_Model Answer_:
```
Original: m_total = 50 kg, m_empty = 30 kg, m_battery = 5 kg, m_payload = 15 kg
New: m_payload = 25 kg, m_total = 60 kg

Power scales with mass:
P_new = P_original × (60/50) = 1.1 × 1.2 = 1.32 kW

Flight time:
t_new = 1.6 / 1.32 = 1.21 hours = 73 min

Range:
R_new = 72 × 1.21 = 87 km

Reduction: 104 km → 87 km (16% range reduction for 67% payload increase) ✓
This is the m-R tradeoff!
```

### 21.2 Drill Set B: Motor Selection

**Drill B1**: Select VTOL motors for 60 kg MTOW with 2:1 thrust-to-weight ratio.

_Model Answer_:
```
Required total thrust (for 2:1 T/W):
T_total = 60 kg × 2 = 120 kg thrust

For 4-motor quadplane:
T_per_motor = 120 / 4 = 30 kg per motor

Select motor: 5008 380KV with 18" prop
Static thrust: ~8-9 kg each
4 motors: ~34 kg total

CHECK: 34 kg / 60 kg = 0.57 T/W at 50% throttle
       At 100% throttle: ~68 kg thrust
       T/W max = 68/60 = 1.13 (need larger motors!)

REVISE: Select 5010 380KV with 20" prop
Static thrust: ~12 kg each
4 motors: ~48 kg total, T/W = 0.8 at 50%
At 100%: ~60+ kg thrust, T/W max ≈ 1.6 ✓

Acceptable: T/W 1.6 provides safe hover margin ✓
```

## Design Pattern Summary: Six Systems Compared

| System | Main Flow | Independence | Environment | Unique Challenge |
|--------|-----------|--------------|-------------|------------------|
| Mixing Tap | Material (water) | V̇ ↔ ϑ | Indoor, benign | Pressure coupling |
| UAV Catapult | Energy (stored) | v ↔ θ | Outdoor, static | Gravity on rail |
| Tethered Drone | Energy + Signal | P ↔ h | Outdoor, altitude | Tether weight, I²R |
| Training Grenade | Energy (delayed) | R ↔ S | Thrown, field | Arming sequence, time |
| Target USV | Energy + Multi-signal | v ↔ σ | Open sea, harsh | Sea state, multi-sensor |
| **Transport UAV** | **MATERIAL (cargo)** | **m ↔ R** | **3D airspace** | **Variable CG, wind** |

**Universal Pattern**: All six systems require **function structure variants** to achieve independence, followed by **working principle selection** and **VDI 2225 evaluation**. The Transport UAV is unique in being **MATERIAL-dominant** - its primary purpose is moving physical cargo, with energy as the supporting flow.
