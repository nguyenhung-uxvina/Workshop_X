## 1. Executive Summary

### 1.1 Project Context

The Tethered Drone (also called Persistent Surveillance Drone or Giám sát Neo buộc) is a multirotor UAV connected to a ground station via a power/data tether, enabling extended flight duration (8-24+ hours) for continuous surveillance operations. Unlike battery-powered drones limited to 30-60 minutes, tethered systems provide "unlimited" endurance for persistent overwatch.

**Key Parallel to Section 6.6 Examples**:

| Aspect | Mixing Tap | UAV Catapult | Tethered Drone |
|--------|------------|--------------|----------------|
| Main flow type | **Material** (water) | **Energy** (kinetic, stored) | **Energy + Signal** (continuous) |
| Independence requirement | V̇ (flow) ↔ ϑ (temp) | v (velocity) ↔ θ (angle) | **P (power) ↔ h (altitude)** |
| Function challenge | Meter before/after mix | Store before release | **Transmit through tether** |
| Unique constraint | Pressure coupling | Gravity on rail | **Tether tension + wind load** |
| VDI 2225 decision | Solution B | Variant B (Hybrid) | To be determined |

### 1.2 Design Objectives Summary

| Objective | Specification | Priority |
|-----------|---------------|----------|
| Continuous flight duration | ≥8 hours (target 24h) | **DEMAND** |
| Operating altitude | 50-150 m above ground | **DEMAND** |
| Payload capacity | ≥3 kg (EO/IR camera) | **DEMAND** |
| Wind resistance | Up to 15 m/s (Force 7) | **DEMAND** |
| Power transmission | Ground power to drone | **DEMAND** |
| Data transmission | HD video + telemetry downlink | **DEMAND** |
| Indigenous manufacturing | >60% local content | **WISH** |
| Cost target | <$80,000 USD per system | **WISH** |

### 1.3 Key Results Preview

Following the complete 8-step process:
- **Function Structure Selected**: Independent power and altitude control with combined tether management
- **Working Principle Group Selected**: High-voltage DC tether with fiber optic data
- **Winning Solution Variant**: "HVDC + Fiber Hybrid Tether" (Variant B)
- **Next Steps**: Tether cable design and power electronics optimization

### 3.3 Physical Relationships

*Following the pattern of Figure 6.29*

**Power Requirement**:
```
Propulsion power (hover):
P_hover = (m × g)^1.5 / √(2 × ρ × A)  (momentum theory)

For m = 15 kg (drone + payload), rotor area A = 0.5 m²:
P_hover ≈ 1,800 W at sea level

With margin for maneuvering and wind:
P_required = 1.5 × P_hover = 2,700 W ≈ 3 kW

Power losses in tether (I²R at 100m):
For 85% efficiency requirement, tether losses ≤ 15%
P_ground = P_drone / η = 3,000 / 0.85 = 3,530 W input
```

**Tether Power Loss Analysis**:
```
P_loss = I² × R_cable

For P = 3 kW at different voltages:
• At 48V DC:  I = 63A, R for 100m cable, P_loss ≈ 500W (17%)  ✗
• At 400V DC: I = 7.5A, R for 100m cable, P_loss ≈ 7W (0.2%) ✓
• At 800V DC: I = 3.75A, R for 100m cable, P_loss ≈ 2W (0.07%) ✓

Higher voltage → Lower current → Lower losses → Lighter cable
```

**Tether Weight Analysis**:
```
Tether must support its own weight + drone pull:

For 100m tether:
• Copper conductors: ≈3-5 kg
• Fiber optic: ≈0.5 kg
• Strength member (Kevlar): ≈1 kg
• Jacket: ≈1 kg
Total: ≈6-8 kg for 100m (60-80 g/m)

Catenary effect: At 150m, tether weight becomes significant
Drag in wind: F_drag = ½ × ρ × v² × Cd × A_tether
```

**Altitude-Power Independence**:
```
Key relationship:
• Power (P) = f(payload, wind, maneuvers) - independent of altitude
• Altitude (h) = f(tether length, wind deflection) - independent of power

BUT there's a coupling effect:
• At higher altitude, tether weight increases → more power needed
• At higher altitude, wind may be stronger → more power needed

Decoupling strategy: Over-size power system for max altitude case
```

### 3.4 Independence Requirements

*Directly paralleling the mixing tap's V̇-ϑ independence and catapult's v-θ independence*

**Critical Insight**: The tethered drone requires:

| Variable | Symbol | Requirement |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Power delivery | P | Adjustable based on flight demand |
| Operating altitude | h | Adjustable 50-200 m independently |

**Independence Conditions**:

1. **Upon changing altitude setting (S_h)**: 
   - Power system must auto-adjust for increased tether weight/drag
   - Data transmission quality must remain unchanged

2. **Upon changing power load (payload change)**:
   - Altitude should remain stable
   - Data link should not degrade

3. **Coupling Effects to Avoid**:
   - Voltage drop affecting control stability at low power
   - Tether tension affecting data transmission (fiber strain)
   - Wind-induced oscillation corrupting video

### 3.5 Alternative System Concepts Considered

*Following the justified dismissal pattern*

| Alternative | Consideration | Decision |
|-------------|---------------|----------|
| Battery-only drone | No tether, 30-60 min endurance | **Dismissed**: Doesn't meet persistence requirement |
| Fuel cell drone | Hydrogen power, 4-6 hr endurance | **Dismissed**: Hydrogen logistics complex |
| Aerostat/balloon | Helium-filled, tethered | **Dismissed**: Larger footprint, helium supply |
| Wireless power transfer | Microwave/laser beaming | **Dismissed**: Technology immature, safety concerns |
| Hybrid battery + tether | Battery primary, tether charging | Considered as option |

**Justified Selection**: 
> Ground-powered tethered multirotor selected as proven approach for persistent surveillance in defense applications (used by multiple militaries including US, Israel, European forces).

## 4. Step 3: Establishing Function Structures

### 4.1 Subfunction Identification

The overall function can be decomposed into:
- **Convert power (ground)**: Transform input power to transmission voltage
- **Transmit power**: Deliver power through tether
- **Convert power (drone)**: Transform to propulsion voltage
- **Manage tether**: Payout, tension, storage
- **Generate lift**: Motor/propeller thrust
- **Stabilize position**: Flight controller
- **Transmit data down**: Video and telemetry
- **Receive commands up**: Control signals
- **Backup power**: Emergency battery

## 6. Step 5: Selecting Working Principles

### 6.1 Working Principle Analysis for Power Path

**HVDC (400V) Analysis**:

```
Power: P = V × I = 400V × 7.5A = 3,000W

Cable resistance (100m, AWG 18 copper):
R = ρ × L / A = 0.017 × 100 / 0.82 = 2.07 Ω

Power loss: P_loss = I² × R = 7.5² × 2.07 = 116W (3.9%)

Total efficiency: 96.1% ✓ (exceeds 85% requirement)

Cable weight: ≈0.05 kg/m × 100m = 5 kg for power conductors
```

**LVDC (48V) Analysis** (for comparison):

```
Power: P = V × I = 48V × 62.5A = 3,000W

Cable resistance (100m, AWG 6 copper - much thicker):
R = ρ × L / A = 0.017 × 100 / 13.3 = 0.128 Ω

Power loss: P_loss = I² × R = 62.5² × 0.128 = 500W (16.7%)

Efficiency: 83.3% ✗ (below 85% requirement)

Cable weight: ≈0.15 kg/m × 100m = 15 kg for power conductors
```

## 10. Skill 1: Feynman Technique Analysis

### 10.1 Concept: "Energy-Signal Hybrid Flow Design"

#### 60-Second Explanation (ELI12)

Imagine a super-long extension cord that also carries a phone line, attached to a flying robot!

The tethered drone is like having:
1. **A power cord** that goes up into the sky (electricity flows UP to the drone)
2. **A video cable** that brings pictures down (data flows DOWN to you)
3. **A leash** that keeps the drone from flying away (it's attached!)

The tricky part: Making all three things work together in ONE thin cable that doesn't weigh too much or tangle up!

#### Everyday Analogy

Think of a vacuum cleaner with a long cord:
- **Vacuum** = Drone (needs power to work)
- **Power cord** = Tether (brings electricity)
- **The cord getting tangled** = The problem we're solving!

The difference: Our "vacuum cleaner" is FLYING, so the cord has to be super light and not pull it down.

#### Defense Application Insight

This pattern appears across defense systems:
- **Towed Target**: Energy flows TO target (via tow cable tension)
- **Submarine periscope**: Signal flows through flexible mast
- **Tank gun stabilization**: Power and signals through turret slip ring

### 10.2 Concept: "P-h Independence = Decoupling Power and Position"

#### 60-Second Explanation

In the tethered drone, you want to:
- **Change altitude** (go higher or lower) WITHOUT messing up the power supply
- **Handle power demands** (wind gusts, payload changes) WITHOUT the drone dropping

This is like being able to adjust your TV volume WITHOUT changing the channel, and changing the channel WITHOUT affecting the volume. Simple for a remote control, but tricky when power and position are connected by the SAME cable!

**Solution**: Design separate "paths" for power and altitude control:
- Power path: Regulated voltage that auto-adjusts
- Altitude path: Tether length that mechanically sets height
- Data path: Fiber optic that ignores both!

### 10.3 Understanding Check Questions

**Basic**:
1. What are the three main "flows" in a tethered drone system?
2. Why is high voltage (400V) used instead of low voltage (48V)?

**Application**:
3. If the wind increases from 5 m/s to 15 m/s, what happens to power demand? Does altitude change?

**Edge Case**:
4. What happens if the tether breaks? What system kicks in?

## 21. Skill 12: Targeted Drill Exercises

### 21.1 Drill Set A: Power Calculations

**Drill A1**: Calculate power loss in a 150m tether at 48V delivering 3 kW. Cable is AWG 10 copper.

_Model Answer_:
```
I = P/V = 3000/48 = 62.5 A
R = 0.00328 Ω/m × 150m × 2 (round trip) = 0.984 Ω
P_loss = I²R = 62.5² × 0.984 = 3,843 W

This exceeds the power being delivered! System fails.
Conclusion: 48V is unsuitable for 150m at 3 kW.
```

**Drill A2**: Same scenario at 400V. Is it acceptable?

_Model Answer_:
```
I = P/V = 3000/400 = 7.5 A
R = 0.00328 Ω/m × 150m × 2 = 0.984 Ω (same cable)
P_loss = I²R = 7.5² × 0.984 = 55.4 W

Efficiency = 3000/(3000+55.4) = 98.2% ✓

400V is highly suitable.
```

### 21.2 Drill Set B: Tether Weight

**Drill B1**: Calculate total tether weight for 100m including:
- Power conductors: 0.03 kg/m
- Fiber: 0.01 kg/m
- Kevlar strength member: 0.015 kg/m
- Jacket: 0.01 kg/m

_Model Answer_:
```
Total per meter = 0.03 + 0.01 + 0.015 + 0.01 = 0.065 kg/m = 65 g/m
For 100m: 6.5 kg

This is within the target of 60-80 g/m ✓
```

### 21.3 Drill Set C: Function Structure

**Drill C1**: Draw what happens if power and data share the same conductors (not independent paths). What's the failure mode?

_Model Answer_: 
- Power fluctuations cause data corruption
- EMI from DC-DC switching injects into data
- If power fails, data also fails immediately
- Cannot diagnose problems independently
