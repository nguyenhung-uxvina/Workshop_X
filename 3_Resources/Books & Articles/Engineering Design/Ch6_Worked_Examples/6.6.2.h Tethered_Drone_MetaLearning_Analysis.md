# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

## Core Design Paradigm: POWER-TETHER-CENTERED DESIGN

**Tethered Drone is fundamentally POWER-TETHER-CENTERED** - unlike free-flying drones (battery-limited), target USVs (autonomy-centered), or target UAVs (signature-centered).

**The Essential Insight**: The physical tether cable that connects ground station to UAV is THE DEFINING constraint - it simultaneously ENABLES (unlimited flight time via continuous power) and CONSTRAINS (limited operational radius, altitude ceiling, cable management complexity).

**Core Trade-off**: Endurance vs. Mobility
- Free-flying drone: 20-45 minutes endurance, unlimited mobility
- Tethered drone: 4-50+ hours endurance, constrained to tether radius

**Essential Problem Statement** (Solution-Neutral):
> "PROVIDE PERSISTENT ELEVATED OBSERVATION PLATFORM FOR EXTENDED DURATION SURVEILLANCE OPERATIONS, ENSURING SAFE CONTINUOUS POWER DELIVERY AND DATA TRANSMISSION WHILE MAINTAINING STABLE HOVER IN ADVERSE CONDITIONS"

# SKILL 1: FEYNMAN TECHNIQUE - EXPLANATIONS AT MULTIPLE LEVELS

## Level 1: ELI5 (Explain Like I'm 5)

**"Balloon Drone on a Magic String"**

Imagine a toy helicopter that has a very special string attached to it. This string is like a magic rope that gives the helicopter electricity from the ground, so it never runs out of power! Regular toy helicopters stop flying when their battery dies (about 20 minutes), but this one can fly ALL DAY because electricity keeps flowing up the magic string.

The string also carries the video from the camera on the helicopter down to a TV screen, so you can see everything the helicopter sees. It's like having a really tall flagpole with eyes that can look around!

**Three Important Parts**:
1. **The Flying Part** (hexacopter) - has 6 spinning blades to stay up
2. **The Magic String** (tether cable) - carries electricity UP and video DOWN
3. **The Box on Ground** (ground station) - makes electricity and shows the video

## Level 2: ELI12 (Middle School)

**"Power Cord in the Sky"**

A tethered drone is like a drone that's plugged into a wall outlet - but the outlet is on the ground and the wire goes up into the sky! This solves the biggest problem with regular drones: they can only fly for about 30 minutes before their battery dies.

**How it works**:
1. A ground station converts AC power (from grid or generator) to high-voltage DC (400-600V)
2. This high voltage travels UP the thin cable (less current = thinner wire = less weight)
3. The drone has a converter that changes it back to what the motors need (48V)
4. Video and data travel DOWN the same cable (or via radio backup)

**Why high voltage?** Physics! Power = Voltage × Current. If you need 1500W:
- At 50V: You need 30 Amps (thick, heavy wire)
- At 500V: You need only 3 Amps (thin, light wire)

The cable also has Kevlar fibers for strength (so it won't break) and has to be managed carefully so it doesn't tangle.

**Critical Design Constraints**:

1. **Power Transmission Efficiency**: Must deliver ≥1500W continuous at ≤5% line loss over 60m
   - Cable resistance: R = ρL/A ≈ 0.5Ω for AWG16 copper at 60m
   - At 500VDC, 3A: P_loss = I²R = 9 × 0.5 = 4.5W (0.3% loss) ✓

2. **Cable Weight Budget**: Tether must not exceed ~5% of UAV thrust capacity
   - Target: ≤300g for 60m cable
   - Achieved: ~4-5mm OD composite cable at ~5g/m = 300g ✓

3. **Safety System**: Must handle tether failure gracefully
   - Onboard backup battery: 4S 3000mAh LiPo (≥2 min hover)
   - Tether break detection: Voltage/current monitoring, ≤3s to auto-land
   - Auto-switch latency: ≤100ms seamless transition

4. **Wind Stability**: Must maintain position in 10 m/s wind
   - Thrust-to-weight ratio: ≥1.8:1
   - Hexacopter provides motor redundancy (can fly on 5 motors)

## Level 4: Expert (System Engineer)

**"High-Voltage Power-Over-Tether Persistent ISR Platform"**

The tethered drone represents a specific system architecture optimized for the surveillance mission profile where persistence time dominates over mobility. The key innovation is the high-voltage DC power transmission architecture that enables continuous power delivery while minimizing tether mass and aerodynamic drag.

**Power Architecture Analysis**:

The system employs a two-stage power conversion topology:
1. **Ground-side AC-DC**: PFC-enabled switching PSU, 220VAC → 500VDC
2. **Air-side DC-DC**: High-efficiency buck converter, 500VDC → 48VDC (motor bus) + auxiliary rails

**Transmission voltage selection rationale**:
- Lower bound (~300V): Limited by DC-DC converter efficiency at higher step-down ratio
- Upper bound (~600V): Limited by cable insulation requirements, connector ratings, safety margins
- Optimal (~400-500V): Balances conductor weight, conversion efficiency, and safety

**Tether Cable Engineering**:

The tether is a composite cable integrating:
- **Power conductors**: Tinned stranded copper, AWG16-18, silicone/PTFE insulated
- **Strength member**: Kevlar/Dyneema core, ≥200kg breaking strength
- **Data path**: Optional coax (video) or fiber optic (high bandwidth)
- **Jacket**: Polyurethane, UV-stabilized, salt-spray resistant

**Critical interface**: Cable terminations must handle:
- Continuous flexing at UAV attachment (swivel bearing)
- Reel drum cycling (≥50mm minimum bend radius)
- Environmental sealing (IP54 minimum)

**Safety System Architecture**:

```
TETHER HEALTH MONITORING:
├── Voltage sensing (ground + air): Detect break/short
├── Current sensing: Detect abnormal load
├── Tension sensing (optional): Detect snag
└── Response: <3s detection → auto-land sequence

BACKUP POWER SYSTEM:
├── Safety battery: 4S LiPo, 3000mAh, 10C discharge
├── Auto-switch: <100ms switchover, <1% voltage dip
├── Capacity: ≥2 min hover at sea level
└── Trickle charge: Maintained from tether power

FLIGHT SAFETY:
├── Motor redundancy: Hexacopter 5-motor flight capable
├── Geofencing: Cylindrical boundary (radius = tether length - margin)
├── Altitude limit: = tether length - 5m safety margin
└── Kill switch: GCS-commanded motor cutoff
```

## Top 10 Critical Design Issues

### Issue 1: Tether Break Response Time

**Problem**: Current spec is "≤3 seconds detection + action" - may be too slow for safe recovery at 50m altitude.

**Analysis**:
- Free-fall from 50m: t = √(2h/g) = √(100/9.8) = 3.2s to impact
- Need margin for controlled descent

**Recommendation**:
- Detection: ≤500ms (voltage monitoring)
- Battery switch: ≤100ms (hardware failover)
- Auto-hover engage: ≤1s from break
- Controlled descent initiation: ≤2s total

### Issue 2: High-Voltage Safety Architecture

**Problem**: 400-600 VDC is lethal. Current requirements address isolation but not comprehensive safety.

**Analysis**:
- IEC 62040-1: Touch-safe voltage is <60VDC
- 500VDC requires Class II double insulation minimum

**Recommendation**:
- Add: "Ground fault detection with <30ms trip"
- Add: "Residual voltage discharge to <50V within 5s of power-off"
- Add: "Interlocked enclosure - power cut on open"

### Issue 3: Cable Management in Wind

**Problem**: 60m cable in 10 m/s wind creates significant drag and can oscillate.

**Analysis**:
- Cable drag force: F = 0.5 × ρ × Cd × D × L × V²
- For 5mm cable at 10 m/s: F ≈ 15N lateral force
- Oscillation can couple with UAV flight dynamics

**Recommendation**:
- Add: "Cable angle from vertical ≤30° at max wind"
- Add: "Tether tension maintained 5-50N during flight"
- Add: "No resonant oscillation in operating wind range"

### Issue 4: Backup Battery Thermal Management

**Problem**: LiPo battery sitting in hot, humid tropical environment may degrade or pose fire risk.

**Analysis**:
- LiPo safe operating: 0°C to 45°C
- Tropical ambient: Up to 40°C + solar heating
- Trickle charging generates heat

**Recommendation**:
- Add: "Battery temperature monitoring with charge cutoff at 50°C"
- Add: "Battery compartment ventilation"
- Add: "Storage temperature limit 25°C for long life"

### Issue 5: Motor Redundancy Verification

**Problem**: Hexacopter claims 5-motor flight capability but no verification requirement.

**Analysis**:
- 5-motor mode requires specific flight controller support
- Not all hexacopter frame geometries support equal motor-out scenarios

**Recommendation**:
- Add: "Demonstrate controlled hover on any 5 of 6 motors"
- Add: "Motor failure detection ≤1s"
- Add: "Automatic compensation engage ≤2s"
- Add: "Audio/visual alert on motor failure"

### Issue 6: Connector Reliability

**Problem**: Tether connectors undergo repeated mating cycles and environmental exposure.

**Analysis**:
- Expected 500+ mating cycles over system life
- Salt spray, humidity, UV exposure
- Power connectors carry 500V at 5A

**Recommendation**:
- Add: "Power connector rated 1000 mating cycles minimum"
- Add: "Connector IP67 when mated"
- Add: "Positive locking mechanism"
- Add: "Keyed to prevent mis-mating"

### Issue 7: GPS Interference from Power System

**Problem**: High-frequency switching in DC-DC converters can interfere with GPS receiver.

**Analysis**:
- Switching frequencies 100kHz-1MHz overlap with GPS harmonics
- Conducted emissions travel through power lines
- Radiated emissions from air module close to GPS antenna

**Recommendation**:
- Add: "GPS position accuracy ≤5m CEP with power system operating"
- Add: "Minimum 150mm separation between air module and GPS antenna"
- Add: "Ferrite filters on air module output cables"

### Issue 8: Night Operations Lighting

**Problem**: Current spec mentions "LED position lights" but no detail on compliance or visibility.

**Analysis**:
- Vietnamese aviation regulations may require specific lighting
- SAR operations need high visibility
- Lighting adds power consumption

**Recommendation**:
- Add: "Strobe light visible 3km at night"
- Add: "Position lights: Red (left), Green (right), White (rear)"
- Add: "Lighting power consumption ≤10W total"

### Issue 9: Video Latency Chain

**Problem**: Spec says "≤200ms video latency" but doesn't specify measurement point.

**Analysis**:
- Total latency = capture + encode + transmit + decode + display
- Each stage adds delay
- Wired tether should enable lower latency than specified

**Recommendation**:
- Add: "Glass-to-glass latency ≤200ms"
- Add: "Encode latency ≤50ms (H.264 low-latency mode)"
- Add: "Wired data path preferred over RF for latency"

### Issue 10: Ground Station Stability

**Problem**: Ground station operating outdoors needs stability in wind and on uneven surfaces.

**Analysis**:
- Tether tension creates overturning moment
- Wind on station structure adds load
- Reel operation causes vibration

**Recommendation**:
- Add: "Ground station stable with 50N tether pull at 45° angle"
- Add: "Leveling feet for ±5° ground slope"
- Add: "Anchor points for tie-down in high wind"

# SKILL 7: MNEMONIC CREATOR

## Formula Mnemonics

### Power Transmission Formula: "CÔNG-ÁP-DÒNG"
```
P = V × I
Công suất = Áp × Dòng (Power = Voltage × Current)
```

**Memory aid**: "Công việc cần có ÁP lực và DÒNG chảy"
(Work needs pressure and flow)

### Line Loss Formula: "DÒNG-VUÔNG-ĐIỆN-TRỞ"
```
P_loss = I² × R
Tổn hao = Dòng² × Điện trở
```

**Memory aid**: "Dòng điện chạy nhanh (bình phương), va vào điện trở"

### Thrust-to-Weight Formula: "LỰC-TRỌNG-HAI"
```
T/W ≥ 1.8 for tethered operation
```

**Memory aid**: "Lực đẩy gần GẤP ĐÔI trọng lượng" (Thrust nearly DOUBLE the weight)

### Cable Voltage Drop: "DÒNG-DÀI-TIẾT-ĐIỆN"
```
V_drop = I × R = I × (ρ × L / A)
V_drop = Dòng × (Điện trở suất × Dài / Tiết diện)
```

**Memory aid**: "DÒNG chảy xa (DÀI), qua ống nhỏ (TIẾT diện), mất ĐIỆN áp"

# SKILL 9: SYSTEMS MAPPER

## System Boundary Definition

### INSIDE System Boundary (Design Scope)

**Ground Station Subsystem**:
- AC-DC Power Supply Unit (PSU)
- Cable Reel System (drum, slip ring, tensioner)
- Ground Control Station (computer, display, controls)
- Safety Monitoring System (voltage, current, temperature)
- Enclosure and Environmental Protection

**Tether Cable Subsystem**:
- Power Conductors (2× copper, insulated)
- Strength Member (Kevlar/Dyneema core)
- Data Path (optional fiber optic or coax)
- Outer Jacket (PU, UV/salt resistant)
- Connectors (ground and air ends)

**UAV Unit Subsystem**:
- Airframe (hexacopter, carbon fiber)
- Propulsion (6× motors, ESCs, propellers)
- Air Module (DC-DC converter)
- Flight Controller + Sensors
- Gimbal + Camera Payload
- Safety Battery + Switch

### OUTSIDE System Boundary (Given/Constraints)

**Environmental**:
- Air (wind, temperature, humidity)
- Ground (terrain, slope, obstacles)
- Electromagnetic (GPS signals, RF environment)
- Solar (daylight, UV exposure)

**Operational**:
- Power Source (AC grid or generator - given)
- Operator (human - given interface requirements)
- Mission Requirements (surveillance targets - given)
- Regulations (airspace, RF licensing - given)

**Supply Chain**:
- Component Availability (motors, electronics - constrained)
- Manufacturing Capability (Vietnamese industry - constrained)
- Technology Access (export controls - constrained)

### Interface Definitions

**Ground-to-Tether Interface (I1)**:
- Mechanical: Cable attachment to reel (drum termination)
- Electrical: Slip ring (power, ground, optional data)
- Signal: Tension feedback to GCS

**Tether-to-Air Interface (I2)**:
- Mechanical: Cable attachment to UAV (swivel mount)
- Electrical: Connector (power in, data out)
- Signal: Heartbeat signal (tether health)

**Ground-to-Environment Interface (I3)**:
- Power: AC input (220VAC from grid/generator)
- Physical: Ground contact (stability, anchoring)
- Environmental: Enclosure sealing (IP54)

**Air-to-Environment Interface (I4)**:
- Aerodynamic: Rotor thrust vs. air
- Thermal: Motor/electronics cooling
- Electromagnetic: GPS/compass reception
- Optical: Camera field of view

# SKILL 12: TARGETED DRILL MASTER

## Drill Set A: Power Transmission

### A.1: Voltage Selection (Basic)
**Time**: 5 minutes
**Problem**: You need to deliver 1500W continuous power through a 60m tether. Calculate the current required at:
- 50VDC
- 200VDC
- 500VDC

**Solution**:
- At 50V: I = P/V = 1500/50 = **30A**
- At 200V: I = P/V = 1500/200 = **7.5A**
- At 500V: I = P/V = 1500/500 = **3A**

**Insight**: Higher voltage = lower current = thinner cables = less weight

### A.2: Line Loss Calculation (Intermediate)
**Time**: 10 minutes
**Problem**: For AWG 18 copper wire (0.823 Ω/km), calculate power loss for 60m (120m round trip) at each voltage from A.1.

**Solution**:
- Wire resistance: R = 0.823 × 0.120 = **0.099Ω** (round trip)
- At 50V (30A): P_loss = I²R = 900 × 0.099 = **89.1W** (5.9% loss) ❌
- At 200V (7.5A): P_loss = I²R = 56.25 × 0.099 = **5.6W** (0.37% loss) ✓
- At 500V (3A): P_loss = I²R = 9 × 0.099 = **0.9W** (0.06% loss) ✓

**Insight**: Line loss goes down with SQUARE of current

### A.3: Efficiency Chain (Advanced)
**Time**: 15 minutes
**Problem**: Calculate overall system efficiency for VN-TUAV-DEMO-001:
- PSU efficiency: 92%
- Transmission (500V): 99.5%
- Air module: 95%
- Motor+ESC: 85%
- Propeller: 75%

**Solution**:
- Overall = 0.92 × 0.995 × 0.95 × 0.85 × 0.75 = **0.555** = **55.5%**
- For 1000W hover: Input = 1000 / 0.555 = **1802W** from AC

**Insight**: Every stage matters - small improvements compound

## Drill Set B: Cable Design

### B.1: Conductor Sizing (Basic)
**Time**: 10 minutes
**Problem**: Select AWG for 500VDC/5A maximum current with ≤3% voltage drop over 60m.

**Given**: AWG 18 = 0.823 Ω/km, AWG 16 = 0.518 Ω/km, AWG 14 = 0.326 Ω/km

**Solution**:
- Allowed drop = 500V × 3% = **15V**
- Max resistance = V/I = 15/5 = **3Ω** (round trip)
- AWG 18: R = 0.823 × 0.120 = 0.099Ω ✓ (much margin)
- **Select AWG 18** (smallest acceptable, lowest weight)

### B.2: Strength Member Sizing (Intermediate)
**Time**: 15 minutes
**Problem**: Size Kevlar core for tethered hexacopter with:
- UAV weight: 8 kg
- Wind drag on cable: 20N at max wind
- Safety factor: 5:1

**Solution**:
- Static load: 8 × 9.8 = 78.4N
- Wind load: 20N
- Total: 78.4 + 20 = **98.4N**
- Required breaking strength: 98.4 × 5 = **492N**
- Kevlar 29: ~2.8 GPa tensile strength
- For 1mm² cross-section: Breaking = 2800N >> 492N ✓
- **Select: 1mm diameter Kevlar core** (provides ~2000N breaking)

### B.3: Cable Weight Budget (Advanced)
**Time**: 20 minutes
**Problem**: Calculate total cable weight for 60m tether:
- 2× AWG 18 copper (32g/m each)
- 1mm Kevlar core (1.4g/m)
- PU jacket 5mm OD (~15g/m estimated)

**Solution**:
- Copper: 2 × 32 × 60 = 3840g
- Kevlar: 1.4 × 60 = 84g
- Jacket: 15 × 60 = 900g
- Total: **4824g ≈ 4.8kg**

**Check**: Is 4.8kg acceptable?
- UAV max payload capacity: ~2kg
- Cable weight is SIGNIFICANT - affects design!
- May need aluminum conductors or shorter cable

## Drill Set C: Propulsion Sizing

### C.1: Thrust Requirement (Basic)
**Time**: 5 minutes
**Problem**: Calculate required total thrust for:
- UAV dry weight: 6 kg
- Cable weight (suspended): 2.4 kg (half of 4.8kg hangs)
- Payload: 0.5 kg
- Thrust-to-weight ratio: 1.8

**Solution**:
- Total weight: 6 + 2.4 + 0.5 = **8.9 kg**
- Required thrust: 8.9 × 1.8 × 9.8 = **156.7N** = **16 kg thrust**
- Per motor (6 motors): 16/6 = **2.67 kg thrust each**

### C.2: Motor Selection (Intermediate)
**Time**: 15 minutes
**Problem**: Select motors for 2.67 kg thrust each at 48V.

**Given options**:
- T-Motor MN4014 KV400: Max thrust 2.5kg, 400W, η=82%
- T-Motor MN5212 KV340: Max thrust 3.8kg, 550W, η=85%
- SunnySky X4112S KV400: Max thrust 2.3kg, 380W, η=80%

**Solution**:
- MN4014: 2.5kg < 2.67kg ❌ (insufficient)
- MN5212: 3.8kg > 2.67kg ✓ (42% margin)
- X4112S: 2.3kg < 2.67kg ❌ (insufficient)

**Select: T-Motor MN5212** - provides margin for gusts

### C.3: Hover Power (Advanced)
**Time**: 20 minutes
**Problem**: Calculate hover power consumption for the selected propulsion.

**Given**: MN5212 at hover (50% throttle): thrust = 1.9kg, power = 180W per motor

**Solution**:
- Total hover thrust needed: 8.9kg
- Per motor: 8.9/6 = 1.48kg (below 50% throttle point)
- Interpolate: ~140W per motor at 1.48kg thrust
- Total hover power: 6 × 140 = **840W**

**Verify against spec**: VN-TUAV-DEMO-001 requires ≤1000W hover ✓

## Drill Set D: Safety Analysis

### D.1: Backup Battery Sizing (Basic)
**Time**: 10 minutes
**Problem**: Size backup battery for 2 minutes hover at sea level.

**Given**: Hover power = 840W, battery voltage = 14.8V (4S LiPo)

**Solution**:
- Energy needed: 840W × (2/60)h = **28 Wh**
- Current at hover: 840/14.8 = **56.8A**
- Capacity: 56.8A × (2/60)h = **1.89 Ah** minimum
- With 20% margin: 1.89 × 1.2 = **2.27 Ah**
- **Select: 3000 mAh 4S LiPo** (standard size, provides margin)

### D.2: Tether Break Detection (Intermediate)
**Time**: 15 minutes
**Problem**: Design tether break detection logic using voltage and current monitoring.

**Normal operation**: V = 500VDC, I = 3A
**Fault conditions**:
- Open circuit (break): V rises toward PSU voltage, I drops to 0
- Short circuit: V drops, I spikes

**Detection Logic**:
```
IF (V_air > 550V AND I_air < 0.5A) for 500ms THEN
    tether_break = TRUE
    trigger_auto_land()
ENDIF
```

**Response time**: 500ms detection + 100ms switch = **600ms** to backup power

### D.3: FMEA Entry (Advanced)
**Time**: 20 minutes
**Problem**: Complete FMEA entry for "Motor 1 Failure During Hover"

| Field | Entry |
|-------|-------|
| **Failure Mode** | Motor 1 stops during hover |
| **Effect (Local)** | Loss of thrust from position 1 |
| **Effect (System)** | UAV yaws and tilts toward failed motor |
| **Effect (Customer)** | Degraded stability, alarm |
| **Cause** | ESC failure, motor winding short, propeller detach |
| **Severity** | 7 (degraded operation, not crash) |
| **Occurrence** | 3 (rare, <1 per 1000 hours) |
| **Detection** | 8 (immediate - current monitoring) |
| **RPN** | 7 × 3 × 8 = **168** |
| **Mitigation** | Hexacopter 5-motor flight mode, motor current monitoring |
| **Post-Mitigation RPN** | 7 × 3 × 2 = **42** ✓ |
