## Executive Summary

Section 4.1 presents the **General Problem Solving Process** - the foundational methodology that underlies all systematic engineering design. This process consists of six sequential phases: **Confrontation → Information → Definition → Creation → Evaluation → Decision**, connected by iteration loops that allow refinement at progressively higher information levels.

**Core Insight for Defense Engineers:** The problem-solving process is NOT a rigid sequence but a flexible framework with mandatory iteration. The key to efficient design lies in keeping iteration loops small—catching problems early before they cascade into expensive late-stage rework.

## Skill 1: Feynman Technique Analysis 🎓

### 60-Second Explanation (Giải Thích 60 Giây)

Giải quyết vấn đề kỹ thuật giống như chiến dịch quân sự: trước tiên **đối đầu** (confrontation) để hiểu địch, **thu thập thông tin** (information) về địa hình và lực lượng, **xác định** (definition) mục tiêu tác chiến, **sáng tạo** (creation) phương án tấn công, **đánh giá** (evaluation) từng phương án, rồi **quyết định** (decision) phương án tốt nhất. Nếu thất bại, quay lại bước trước với thông tin mới - đây là **vòng lặp iteration**.

### Everyday Analogy (Ví Dụ Hàng Ngày)

**Building a House vs. Designing a Weapon System:**

| House Construction | Weapon System Design |
|:---|:---|
| Confrontation: "I need a house" | "We need a target drone for anti-aircraft training" |
| Information: Climate, budget, family size | Threat parameters, budget, operational requirements |
| Definition: "4-bedroom, 2-bath, under $300K" | "Simulate fighter aircraft, Mach 0.8, 10km range" |
| Creation: Draw floor plans, select materials | Develop propulsion concepts, airframe designs |
| Evaluation: Compare layouts, get quotes | VDI 2225 evaluation, prototype testing |
| Decision: Choose contractor, approve plan | Select final design, commit resources |

### Defense-Specific Example: Target USV Design

**Confrontation:**
- "We need surface targets for naval gunnery training"
- Unknown: What speed? What size? What signature?

**Information Gathering:**
- Study existing systems (Hammerhead, SEALION)
- Analyze Vietnamese naval training requirements
- Research available propulsion technologies

**Definition (Solution-Neutral):**
- "Provide realistic surface target that can be engaged by 12.7mm-76mm weapons within 0-5km range"
- NOT: "Build a fiberglass boat with diesel engine"

**Creation:**
- Generate concepts: Planing hull, SWATH, catamaran
- Vary propulsion: Diesel, electric, hybrid
- Explore control: RF, autonomous, hybrid

**Evaluation:**
- Apply VDI 2225 criteria
- Technical: Speed, maneuverability, signature
- Economic: Development, production, operation costs

**Decision:**
- Select winning concept
- Or iterate if no satisfactory solution found

### Quick Recall Test ✅

1. **Q:** Name the 6 phases of general problem solving in order.
   **A:** Confrontation → Information → Definition → Creation → Evaluation → Decision

2. **Q:** Why is the Definition phase solution-neutral?
   **A:** To avoid premature fixation on one solution; enables broader search for unconventional approaches

3. **Q:** What triggers an iteration loop?
   **A:** When results from a step don't meet objectives but resources permit repetition with expectation of better results

### Common Misconceptions ❌→✅

| Misconception | Correct Understanding |
|:---|:---|
| "Follow the 6 steps rigidly in sequence" | Steps are guidelines; adapt to situation; skip or reorder as needed |
| "Iteration means failure" | Iteration is EXPECTED and NECESSARY; only disaster is large loops spanning entire project |
| "Definition = detailed specification" | Definition is ABSTRACT, solution-neutral problem statement |
| "Evaluation only happens at the end" | Evaluation occurs after EVERY step as progress check |

### Chunk 2: The Six Phases (45 min, ⭐⭐)

**Prerequisites:** Chunk 1

**Core Concepts (6 items):**
1. **Confrontation** - Elucidating known vs. unknown
2. **Information** - Gathering task constraints, solutions, similar problems
3. **Definition** - Abstract, solution-neutral problem statement
4. **Creation** - Generating and varying solutions
5. **Evaluation** - Checking progress toward objective
6. **Decision** - Proceed, iterate, or stop

**Mnemonic: "CIDCED"** (Confrontation-Information-Definition-Creation-Evaluation-Decision)
- Vietnamese: **"Chạm-Tin-Định-Chế-Đánh giá-Định đoạt"**
- Memory hook: "CHẠM vào TIN tức, ĐỊNH hướng CHẾ tạo, ĐÁNH GIÁ rồi ĐỊNH ĐOẠT"

**Defense Application Matrix:**

| Phase | Target UAV | LOMAH System | 12.7mm RCWS |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Confrontation | "Need realistic aerial threat" | "Need miss-distance measurement" | "Need stabilized fire platform" |
| Information | Threat aircraft specs, training doctrine | Acoustic/radar sensing options | Existing weapon stations, vehicle interfaces |
| Definition | "Simulate aircraft approach at realistic speeds and altitudes" | "Measure projectile trajectory within ±0.5m at 0-1000m range" | "Enable accurate fire from moving vehicle" |

### Chunk 3: Iteration Dynamics (45 min, ⭐⭐⭐)

**Prerequisites:** Chunks 1-2

**Core Concepts (5 items):**
1. Iteration as progressive refinement
2. TOTE model parallel (Test-Operate-Test-Exit)
3. Higher information level each cycle
4. Small loops good, large loops catastrophic
5. Reasons: complex interrelationships, need forward information

**Critical Understanding:**

```
Small Iteration (GOOD):
Creation → Evaluation → [not satisfied] → Creation (improved)
Total time: 2-3 weeks

Large Iteration (DISASTER):
Confrontation → ... → Detail Design → [not satisfied] → Confrontation
Total time: 6-12 months lost
```

**Defense Example - UAV Catapult:**

*Small Iteration (Desirable):*
- Create: Pneumatic launch concept
- Evaluate: Insufficient velocity (target: 25 m/s, achieved: 18 m/s)
- Iterate: Increase pressure, modify rail length
- Re-evaluate: 26 m/s achieved ✓

*Large Iteration (Catastrophic):*
- Complete pneumatic catapult design
- Build prototype
- Discover: Acceleration exceeds UAV structural limits
- Must restart with bungee or electromagnetic concept
- Lost: 4 months design work, $30,000 materials

**Strategies for Small Loops:**
1. Early prototyping (physical/virtual)
2. Front-loading critical decisions
3. Requirements validation before creation
4. Multi-disciplinary reviews at each phase

**Practice Exercise:**
For the Transport (Cargo) Drone project, identify 3 potential large iteration risks and propose early-detection strategies.

### Chunk 4: Decision Making Process (30 min, ⭐⭐)

**Prerequisites:** Chunks 1-3

**Core Concepts (4 items):**
1. Four decision paths (proceed, stop, iterate, pivot)
2. Resource-constrained repetition
3. Objective modification option
4. Cascading through phases

**Decision Logic Flowchart:**

```
Results meet objective?
├── YES → Proceed to next step
└── NO → Resources permit iteration?
         ├── YES → Expect good results?
         │         ├── YES → Repeat at higher info level
         │         └── NO → Stop development
         └── NO → Stop development

Side branch: Results interesting for modified objectives?
├── YES → Investigate objective change or alternative application
└── NO → Archive learnings for future projects
```

**Defense Application - Radar-IR Target Simulation:**

*Scenario:* Thermal signature generation concept produces only 30% of required radiance (objective: 100% of target aircraft signature).

*Decision Analysis:*
1. Results meet objective? NO (30% < 100%)
2. Resources permit iteration? YES (budget remaining, schedule has 2 months slack)
3. Expect good results? EVALUATE:
   - Root cause: Emitter material limitation
   - Known solutions: Higher-temp materials, larger area, active heating
   - Expected improvement: 80-120% achievable with material change
   - Decision: **ITERATE** with new emitter material

*Alternative Scenario:* If physics limit is 40%, no material can achieve 100%
- Decision: **Stop** or **modify objectives** (accept 40% for basic training, develop alternative for advanced training)

**Practice Exercise:**
The Machine Gun Mount System prototype shows excessive vibration (3x specification). Apply the decision flowchart to determine next action.

## Skill 3: Design Review Criteria 🔍

### Phase Gate Checklist: General Problem Solving

**Gate 1: Confrontation Complete**
- [ ] Known/unknown explicitly documented
- [ ] Designer experience level assessed
- [ ] Similar problem solutions researched
- [ ] Confidence level for solution finding estimated
- [ ] Stakeholders identified and consulted

**Gate 2: Information Adequate**
- [ ] Task constraints documented
- [ ] Solution principles surveyed
- [ ] Prior art/patents reviewed
- [ ] Requirements nature clarified (D vs W)
- [ ] Information gaps identified with mitigation plan

**Gate 3: Definition Quality**
- [ ] Problem stated on abstract plane
- [ ] Essential problems (crux) identified
- [ ] Solution-neutral language used
- [ ] Main constraints articulated
- [ ] Objectives measurable and testable

**Gate 4: Creation Completeness**
- [ ] Multiple solution variants generated (>3)
- [ ] Solutions varied using methodical guidelines
- [ ] Combinations explored systematically
- [ ] Novel approaches considered (not just obvious)
- [ ] Physical/functional feasibility verified

**Gate 5: Evaluation Rigor**
- [ ] Criteria aligned with objectives
- [ ] Weighting justified and documented
- [ ] All variants assessed against same criteria
- [ ] Technical AND economic criteria included
- [ ] Sensitivity analysis performed

**Gate 6: Decision Transparency**
- [ ] Decision logic documented
- [ ] Stakeholder input incorporated
- [ ] Risk assessment completed
- [ ] Resource implications understood
- [ ] Iteration/stop/proceed rationale clear

### Defense System Application: Small Arms Simulator

| Gate | Critical Review Questions |
|:---|:---|
| Confrontation | Have we studied all existing simulators (FATS, VirTra, MILO)? What's our unique value proposition? |
| Information | Do we understand Vietnamese marksmanship doctrine? What feedback mechanisms do shooters need? |
| Definition | Have we defined "realistic training" without specifying laser/optical/pneumatic technology? |
| Creation | Did we explore non-obvious solutions (haptic, audio, VR hybrid)? |
| Evaluation | Did we weight "training effectiveness" separately from "cost per shot"? |
| Decision | If our solution costs 2x competitors but provides 1.5x training value, is that acceptable? |

### MNEMONIC: Decision Logic (4 Paths)

**Type:** Rhyme  
**Mnemonic:**

```
Kết quả ĐẠT → tiếp TỤC đi,
Kết quả CHƯA → xét TÀI nguyên:
  - CÓ tài, KỲ vọng → lặp LẠI ngay,
  - KHÔNG tài hoặc HẾT hy vọng → DỪNG.
```

**Translation:**
- Results PASS → continue forward
- Results FAIL → check resources:
  - Resources available AND promising → iterate
  - No resources OR no hope → stop

|:---|:---|

| - Budget for redesign exists | CÓ → expect improvement with thicker cable? |
| - Better cable material exists | KỲ vọng → iterate design |
| - No suitable material found | HẾT hy vọng → stop development |

## Skill 9: Systems Mapping 🗺️

### Stock-Flow Diagram: Design Problem Solving

**System Boundary:**
- INSIDE: Design team activities, decisions, deliverables
- OUTSIDE: Customer requirements (given), budget constraints (fixed)
- INTERFACES: Requirements input, deliverable output, management reviews

**Key Stocks:**
1. **Knowledge Stock** - Understanding of problem and solutions
2. **Solution Variants Stock** - Number of viable alternatives
3. **Design Maturity Stock** - Completeness of selected design
4. **Resource Stock** - Time, budget, personnel remaining

**Key Flows:**
| Flow | Rate | Affects Stock |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Information acquisition | +Knowledge/week | Knowledge ↑ |
| Knowledge decay | -Knowledge/month without use | Knowledge ↓ |
| Variant generation | +Variants/iteration | Solution Variants ↑ |
| Variant elimination | -Variants/evaluation | Solution Variants ↓ |
| Design refinement | +Maturity/iteration | Design Maturity ↑ |
| Resource consumption | -Resources/week | Resource Stock ↓ |

### Feedback Loops in Problem Solving

**R1: Learning Acceleration Loop (Reinforcing)**
```
More iterations → More knowledge gained → Better questions asked →
Faster information finding → More iterations possible [GROWTH]
```
*Defense Application:* In Target UAV development, each flight test increases team knowledge, enabling smarter next-iteration decisions, accelerating overall learning.

**B1: Resource Constraint Loop (Balancing)**
```
More iterations → More resources consumed → Fewer resources remaining →
Pressure to decide → Fewer iterations [STABILIZING]
```
*Defense Application:* RCWS development budget limits how many stabilization concepts can be prototyped; eventually team must decide with available information.

**B2: Complexity Growth Loop (Balancing)**
```
More solution variants → More evaluation effort required →
Longer evaluation time → Pressure to reduce variants [STABILIZING]
```
*Defense Application:* Morphological matrix for Training Grenade yields 50+ combinations; team must systematically eliminate to manageable number.

**R2: Rework Spiral Loop (Reinforcing - Negative)**
```
Large iteration loop → Schedule pressure → Skip early phases →
Poor definition → Wrong creation → Large iteration loop [COLLAPSE]
```
*Defense Application:* Rushing Naval Weapon Simulator definition leads to wrong sensor selection, requiring complete restart—classic large iteration disaster.

### Leverage Points for Efficient Problem Solving

| Level | Intervention | Impact | Application |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| L12 | Adjust iteration budget | Low | Allow 20% more iterations |
| L9 | Reduce information delay | Medium | Establish rapid prototyping capability |
| L6 | Create information visibility | High | Real-time progress dashboards |
| L4 | Change evaluation rules | High | Require Confrontation sign-off before Creation |
| L3 | Change goal | Very High | "Minimize iteration" vs "Complete quickly" |

```
Long Break: 20 min
```
*Total: 160 min for comprehensive information gathering*

**Definition Phase (Convergent Thinking)**
```
Session 1: 25 min - Draft solution-neutral statement
Break: 5 min
Session 2: 25 min - Review for solution bias
Break: 5 min
Session 3: 15 min - Finalize and validate
```
*Total: 75 min for quality definition*

**Creation Phase (Creative Generation)**
```
Session 1: 30 min - Free brainstorming (no criticism)
Break: 10 min
Session 2: 30 min - Systematic variation (morphological)
Break: 10 min
Session 3: 30 min - Combination exploration
Break: 10 min
Session 4: 20 min - Documentation
```
*Total: 140 min for thorough creation*

**Evaluation Phase (Analytical)**
```
Session 1: 25 min - Establish criteria and weights
Break: 5 min
Session 2: 35 min - Score variants systematically
Break: 10 min
Session 3: 20 min - Sensitivity analysis
Break: 5 min
Session 4: 15 min - Document and review
```
*Total: 115 min for rigorous evaluation*

**Decision Phase (Critical Judgment)**
```
Session 1: 20 min - Review evaluation results
Break: 5 min
Session 2: 25 min - Stakeholder consultation
Break: 5 min
Session 3: 15 min - Decision documentation
```
*Total: 70 min for well-supported decision*

## Skill 12: Targeted Drill Master 🎯

### Drill Set 1: Phase Recognition (Speed Drill)

**Instructions:** Identify which phase the activity belongs to. Target: <5 seconds per item.

1. "The team reviews competitor products" → **Information**
2. "We need something to detect miss distance" → **Confrontation**
3. "Let's define the essential function without specifying sensors" → **Definition**
4. "Generate 10 different propulsion concepts" → **Creation**
5. "Score each concept against the criteria" → **Evaluation**
6. "Based on the scores, select Concept B" → **Decision**
7. "What do we know about the threat radar frequency?" → **Confrontation**
8. "Consult the patent database for towing mechanisms" → **Information**
9. "The system must track projectiles within specified accuracy" → **Definition**
10. "Combine acoustic with optical sensing" → **Creation**

**Model Answers Available:** Self-check against answers above.

### Drill Set 2: Solution-Neutral Definition Writing

**Instructions:** Rewrite each biased statement as solution-neutral. Time limit: 3 minutes each.

1. **Biased:** "Design a GPS-guided training grenade"
   **Neutral:** "Provide trainees with impact point feedback during grenade training"

2. **Biased:** "Build a pneumatic UAV catapult"
   **Neutral:** "Accelerate UAV from rest to flight speed within limited runway length"

3. **Biased:** "Develop a VR-based naval weapon simulator"
   **Neutral:** "Enable realistic naval engagement training without live ammunition expenditure"

4. **Biased:** "Create a camera-based LOMAH system"
   **Neutral:** "Measure projectile miss-distance from target within specified accuracy for training feedback"

5. **Biased:** "Design an electric servo-stabilized gun mount"
   **Neutral:** "Maintain weapon pointing accuracy despite platform motion"

### Drill Set 3: Iteration Decision Scenarios

**Instructions:** For each scenario, determine the correct decision path. Justify in 2 sentences.

**Scenario A:** Tethered Drone prototype achieves 85% of required endurance. Budget allows 2 more prototypes. Engineering believes battery optimization can reach 100%.
**Decision:** ITERATE (resources available + positive expectation)

**Scenario B:** Target UAV cost estimate is 200% of budget target. No identified cost reduction path. Customer inflexible on budget.
**Decision:** STOP or MODIFY OBJECTIVES (no viable path forward with current objectives)

**Scenario C:** RAMS accuracy meets specification. Schedule has slack. Team sees potential for 20% improvement.
**Decision:** PROCEED (objectives met; improvement is optional enhancement, not required iteration)

**Scenario D:** Transport Drone payload capacity is 70% of requirement. Structural redesign required. Budget exists but timeline would slip 4 months.
**Decision:** EVALUATE cost of delay vs value of meeting spec; may choose to MODIFY OBJECTIVES (accept lower payload) or ITERATE (if payload critical)

### Drill Set 4: Information-Definition Connection

**Instructions:** Given the information list, write a solution-neutral Definition. Time: 5 minutes.

**Information for Machine Gun Mount System:**
- Weapon: 12.7mm (.50 cal), 500 rounds/min rate of fire
- Platform: Patrol boat, ±15° roll typical
- Threat: Small fast boats at 100-2000m
- Constraint: Cannot modify boat structure significantly
- User: Conscripts with 2-week training

**Target Definition:**
"Enable accurate engagement of maneuvering surface targets from a moving platform, operable by personnel with minimal training, within existing platform structural limitations."

*Note: Does NOT specify stabilization method, sight type, or control mechanism*

## Conclusion

Section 4.1's General Problem Solving Process provides the foundational methodology that underlies all systematic engineering design. The key insights are:

1. **Process is Flexible:** The 6 phases (CIDCED) are guidelines, not rigid prescriptions
2. **Definition is Critical:** Solution-neutral problem statements enable broader solution search
3. **Iteration is Expected:** Small loops are normal; large loops are disasters to prevent
4. **Decision Logic is Structured:** 4 clear paths (proceed/iterate/stop/pivot) guide progress

For Vietnamese defense engineering, this framework applies equally to Training Grenades and Naval Weapon Simulators—only the complexity and iteration depth differ. Mastery requires deliberate practice across all 13 meta-learning skills.
