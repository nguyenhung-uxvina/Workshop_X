### 2.1 Stock-Flow Map (4 critical stocks)

**Stock 1: AI Technical Capability**
- Level: Varies by tier | Type: CORE ASSET
- Inflows: Learning (fast if systematic), practice projects (medium), client work (slow but deep)
- Outflows: Technology obsolescence (FAST — 6-12 month cycles), skill decay without practice
- Pattern: **Treadmill** — must keep running to stay in place. dA/dt > dH/dt

**Stock 2: Client Trust & Portfolio**
- Level: Zero for newcomers | Type: CONSTRAINT
- Inflows: Case studies (slow), referrals (slow-medium), content publishing (medium)
- Outflows: Failed projects (INSTANT depletion), market saturation (medium)

**Stock 3: Revenue/Cash**
- Level: Target $1K/month → $10K → $100K | Type: OUTPUT

- Level: Zero initially | Type: MOAT
- Inflows: Each client project adds templates/components (slow compound)
- Outflows: Technology shifts invalidate systems (episodic)
- **This is the real asset** — not skill, but SYSTEMS

## PART 4: FIRST-PRINCIPLES DEBATE

### Debate Point 1: "The money is in architecting AI systems"

**The claim:** Moving from AI user (Tier 1) to AI architect (Tier 4) is the path to $500K+.

**First-principles counter:** For Workshop X, the money is NOT in "architecting AI systems" generically — it's in **AI × Defense Domain Expertise**. A generic AI architect competes with millions globally. A defense AI architect who understands Vietnamese military procurement, recoil fidelity thresholds, and MIL-STD-882E safety requirements competes with maybe 5 people in the country.

**Corrected frame:** Tier 4 for Workshop X = "Defense AI-Native Company" — not "AI-Native Company." The domain constraint IS the moat. Without domain, you're a commodity AI builder racing to the bottom on price.

**Systems integration:** This is the [[Compound Law]] — HELIX (engineering) × FORGE (AI/ACH) × BRIDGE (operations) = compound value. Remove any pillar = zero.

### Debate Point 2: "Tier 3 (Fine-tuning) will be the new baseline in 24 months"

**The claim:** RAG and fine-tuning will be commoditized by 2028.

**Current vs Fundamental test:**
- **Current limitation:** Fine-tuning requires expertise → will tools simplify it? YES — OpenAI, Llama Factory already making it easier
- **Fundamental limitation:** Fine-tuning on DEFENSE DATA requires classified data handling, adversarial robustness testing, and Performance Envelope validation → these are DOMAIN requirements that tools won't automate

**Corrected frame:** Generic Tier 3 WILL be commoditized. **Defense-grade Tier 3 (fine-tuning with MIL-STD validation + classified data + edge deployment)** will remain premium because the DOMAIN requirements are the hard part, not the ML.

### Debate Point 3: "AI-native businesses have near-zero marginal costs"

**The claim:** Software economics → infinite scalability.

**Counter for defense:** Defense products have:
- Non-zero marginal cost: ruggedized hardware, field deployment, operator training
- Non-infinite scalability: each military unit needs customization, physical installation
- Regulatory overhead: each customer/country requires separate validation

**Corrected frame for Workshop X:** ACH products have **software margins on the AI LAYER** (80-95%) but **hardware margins on the physical LAYER** (20-40%). The blended margin is better than pure hardware but NOT "near-zero marginal cost." The compound advantage is in the AI layer IMPROVING over time (R1 data flywheel) while hardware stays constant.

## PART 7: SYNTHESIS

### 7.1 Single Most Important Insight

**Bitblaze's pyramid is correct but INCOMPLETE for Workshop X.** The missing dimension is DOMAIN. Workshop X is already at Tier 2+ in AI skills but at Tier 0 in deployment. The bottleneck is NOT learning more AI — it's DEPLOYING AI on physical products (dP/dt = 0). Bitblaze's 90-day plan won't help because Workshop X doesn't need to learn to build AI wrappers — it needs to deploy AI on Jetson controlling a ballast valve in seawater.

### 7.2 Counter-Intuitive Insights

1. **Workshop X should SKIP Tier 2 saturation worry** — defense AI is regulatory-protected from commoditization
2. **Tier 3 (fine-tuning) matters MORE for WX than Tier 4** — defense-grade fine-tuning is where value concentrates
3. **Physical integration (ACH) > Software architecture** — Bitblaze ignores physical world entirely, but that's WX's competitive advantage
4. **Case studies > courses** for defense — military customers trust demonstrated capability, not training certificates
5. **The LP-1→LP-3→LP-2→LP-5 path already IS the progression** — buying Jetson = entering Tier 3 execution, not just learning

### 7.3 The Three Laws (Workshop X context)

1. **Domain Multiplier:** AI × Defense = Compound. AI × Zero Domain = Commodity.
2. **Saturation Escape:** Regulatory barriers > technical barriers = anti-commodity moat.
3. **Physical Integration:** AI touching atoms > AI touching bits = 10× harder to replicate.
