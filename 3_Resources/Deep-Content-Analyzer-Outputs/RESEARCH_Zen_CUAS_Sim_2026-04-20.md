## DEFINITIVE FINDINGS (from NLM 32-source cross-analysis)

### Finding 1: C-UAS Sim = LAND-BASED ADS OPERATOR TRAINER, NOT Maritime Gunnery
- **Source tier:** A (PRNewswire press release, Janes, bharatshakti) — ★★★ HIGH
- **Evidence:** "designed to train detection and neutralisation workflows" for Zen's "operationally deployed Counter-Unmanned Aircraft System"
- **What it IS:** Console-based training for radar/jammer operators (detect → classify → track → jam → direct hard kill)
- **What it is NOT:** Maritime gunnery trainer, weapon station simulator, or shooting training
- **Hardware:** Likely replicates Zen ADS Data Fusion Command Center (DFCC) — screens showing signal view + map + camera, NOT dome projection or weapon replicas
- **Confidence:** ★★★ HIGH
- **Galaxy candidate:** NO (factual finding, not design principle)

### Finding 2: Zen Has THREE Separate C-UAS Training Products
- **Source tier:** A (multiple sources cross-referenced) — ★★★ HIGH

| Product | Trains | Domain | Hardware | Competition to WX |
|---------|--------|--------|----------|:-----------------:|
| **C-UAS Simulation Solution** | ADS console operators (detect/jam) | Land/base defense | Screen-based DFCC replica | **NONE** |
| **IADCS** | L70/ZU-23 gun crews vs air targets incl. drones | Land (air defense) | Dome projection, gun replica, recoil | **INDIRECT** (different weapon, different platform) |
| **FAC Sim** | Naval crews vs asymmetric threats incl. drones via RWS/MMG | Maritime | 360° projection, 6-DOF, weapon station | **MOST DIRECT** (same paradigm as WX) |

- **Key insight:** The "Counter-UAS Simulation Solution" name is MISLEADING — it's the LEAST threatening of Zen's three C-UAS-adjacent products for WX
- **Confidence:** ★★★ HIGH

### Finding 4: Zen Naval RCWS Sharur (12.7mm) Trains via FAC Sim

- **WX relevance:** FAC Sim already covers 12.7mm naval gunnery training. This is WX's direct competitor — but at $200-500K vs WX's $50-70K
