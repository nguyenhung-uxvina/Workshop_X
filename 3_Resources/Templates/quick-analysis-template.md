## Quick Reference: Leverage Point Hierarchy

**Most Effective (Start Here)**:
- L1: Transcend paradigms (question everything)
- L2: Paradigm shift (change mental model)
- L3: System goals (redefine success)
- L4: Self-organization (enable adaptation)
- L5: System rules (change incentives)
- L6: Information flow (who knows what)

**Moderate Effectiveness**:
- L7: Reinforcing loops (growth/collapse)
- L8: Balancing loops (error correction)
- L9: Delays (feedback speed)

**Least Effective (Last Resort)**:
- L10: Physical structure (expensive, slow)
- L11: Buffer sizes (treats symptoms)
- L12: Parameters (numbers, quotas, budgets)

**Key Principle**: One high-leverage intervention > ten low-leverage tweaks
